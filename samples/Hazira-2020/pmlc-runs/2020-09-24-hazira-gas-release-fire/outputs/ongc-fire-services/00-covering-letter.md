**Subject: Learnings for Fire Services from the Hazira gas terminal vapour release and fire of 24 September 2020: draft pack for your consideration**

Dear Sir / Madam,

In the early hours of 24 September 2020, a condensate leak at the Gas Terminal of the Hazira Gas Processing Complex formed a vapour cloud that ignited 53 minutes after CISF guards first saw it and informed your Fire Station. The fire was contained with no injuries inside the plant: cooling began at about 03:50, the fire was out by about 06:00, and all-clear came at 07:30. We have studied this event closely, and we believe it holds lessons that are specifically useful to fire services.

We are sharing, unsolicited and without charge, a short pack of learnings drawn from that study for ONGC Fire Services:

- **01 Brief for the Head of Fire Services:** the event, five takeaways and three decisions, in two pages.
- **02 Fire Services Learnings:** the full narrative of the night, four learnings within Fire Services' own remit and six at the interfaces, each with a self-check for any ONGC installation.
- **03 Drill Scenarios:** four exercises, each with the Hazira interval as a baseline and a blank for your own target.
- **04 Readiness Indicators:** twenty leading indicators with Hazira baselines.

**How the pack was made.** This is an independent learning analysis, built from publicly available information about the incident and analysed with ProbeMatrix, our investigation method based on the four-level Human Factors Analysis and Classification System. Trained investigators review its AI-assisted analysis. It is **not** ONGC's investigation and does not claim to state ONGC's findings. It is marked as a draft, and it cannot see your fire service's own records. Where those records would answer a question, for example what the crews did between the first call and the start of cooling, the pack says so rather than guessing.

**Our approach to people.** The pack names functions, never individuals or ranks. Its learnings concern the plans, equipment and authority the people on duty were given, not anyone's conduct.

We would welcome any correction from your department, and we would be glad to walk your officers through the pack or help adapt the drill scenarios to a particular installation, at your convenience.
