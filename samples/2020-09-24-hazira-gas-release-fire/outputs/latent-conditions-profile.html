Latent conditions

5

Each condition runs under several separate findings and register learnings. The counts show how widely the investigation found it, not how much it contributed.

High-consequence work went ahead without written guidance, because no one had written the specification, procedure or criteria the work needed.

Missing policy and guidance from supervision (SVI 1.4) recurs in 21 findings, more than any other code. The gaps include the substitute gasket specification [], a return-to-service procedure for the high-pressure meter [], automatic shutdown from gas detection [] and the emergency plan's isolation criteria []. The same code sits under 12 register learnings, including LR-01, LR-02 and LR-07.

Evidence: corroborated
+5 supporting

Risks the site knew about were accepted as normal working, so unsafe conditions became the way tasks were done.

A culture that allowed unsafe tasks (OIC 2.1) has 36 links out, the most of any code, and appears in 14 of the 25 register learnings. It covers locally bought O-rings as a regular practice [], gas detection that only displayed an alarm [], isolation valves with no remote operation [] and a damaged flameproof lamp left in service [].

Evidence: corroborated
+4 supporting

Repeat failures of the same seal were handled as routine repairs, so the warning they gave never reached a reliability review.

Inconsistent risk management (OIC 2.7) recurs in 19 findings and has 24 links out; the maintenance, spares and support programme (OIR 1.6) has 26 links out. Both meet at meter 310FT01: three gasket replacements in about 22 months were not escalated for root-cause action [] [].

Evidence: corroborated

The change procedure existed on paper but was not in use, so a material substitution on a pressure boundary had no change review.

The 2016 management-of-change procedure had not been used since 2018 []. The same condition is coded four ways: missing guidance (SVI 1.4), a culture that allowed unsafe tasks (OIC 2.1), inconsistent risk management (OIC 2.7) and a problem left uncorrected (SVF 3.2).

Evidence: corroborated
+2 supporting

Integrity findings that were measured and reported stayed open for years without a corrective plan.

The Failure to Correct Known Problem row holds 40 findings; operations management (SVF 3.2) recurs 18 times and ineffective action against an identified risk (SVF 3.4) 14 times. The 2017 thickness survey had no corrective plan three years later [], and pitted pipe on a zero-corrosion-allowance line was painted [].
