The event sequence

1

24 September 2020, from CCTV, witness statements and the reconstructed response timeline.

  1. 2020-09-24 02:11CCTV shows the metering skid intact, with no vapour (02:11:43).
  2. 2020-09-24 02:12CCTV shows the vapour leak beginning at the condensate metering skid (02:12:50).
  3. 2020-09-24 02:15A perimeter security post hears noise and sees a vapour cloud from the gas terminal area, and informs the main gate, the fire station and the LPG control room.
  4. 2020-09-24 02:16The gas terminal gate post, which also saw the cloud, informs the CCTV control room and the main gate.
  5. 2020-09-24 02:18The CCTV control room confirms heavy leakage on camera and informs the gas terminal control room.
  6. 2020-09-24 02:20The gas terminal control room hears the annunciator alarm and takes a call about 'some vapour cloud or steam leakage'; staff go outside and find heavy cloud and very poor visibility.
  7. 2020-09-24 02:35The shift in-charges are called from their quarters. One cannot enter the gas control room area for the gas smell; the other instructs that the 36-inch pressure control valve be closed and the control room left, and informs senior managers.
  8. 2020-09-24 03:00CCTV shows vapour all around the skid, even at height (03:00:44).
  9. 2020-09-24 03:01The first emergency isolation valve is closed by hand.
  10. 2020-09-24 03:04The 36-inch pressure control valve is closed.
  11. 2020-09-24 03:08The cloud ignites (03:08:15); seven seconds later the CCTV cable burns and the recording stops.
  12. 2020-09-24 03:10Stopping the offshore supply is ordered, after ignition.
  13. 2020-09-24 03:50Active fire cooling begins, carried by the electric fire-water pumps.
  14. 2020-09-24 06:00The fire is extinguished; cooling continues.
  15. 2020-09-24 07:30All clear.
