## Method lessons

Where the analysis reached levels 3 and 4: it climbed from the seal substitution as an act (level 1) through supervision that let it become normal (level 3) to the absence of any function owning the specification (level 4). The same climb is visible for the pressure test, the isolation decision and the detection design. [FND-8C0BB623; FND-A2048FB8; FND-23139BF9] *(learning: LR-01)*
