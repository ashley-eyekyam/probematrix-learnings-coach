Data quality and limits

  • Draft learnings from an unreviewed analysis: the ProbeMatrix investigation status is in progress and no finding is marked as edited by a person. Validate before adoption.
  • The schedule, the Kirkpatrick levels, the leading indicators and the audit questions are the engine's (effect-plan, from the register); the lagging indicators and what an auditor would see are the analyst's, drawn from each learning's lesson, worst case and recommendations.

Bottom line

  • The plan sets 97 checks for the register's 25 learnings, each counted from the day the organisation adopts the learning: the 11 priority-1 learnings are checked five times in their first 12 months, the other 14 three times; every check is in the schedule below.
  • On Jacobsson's measures, 16 learnings spread to the industry and 9 to the organisation; 19 change the system and 6 (LR-08, LR-12, LR-18, LR-19, LR-20, LR-24) change a procedure, so their effect depends most on the checks of how they are applied.

Recording the checks

1

The organisation records each check in its own verification log, made with PMLC templates <ws> verification: one line per learning, with the check date, the level reached (communicated, understood, applied or effective), the evidence seen and the role that assessed it, all left blank for the organisation. Statuses come only from the organisation; this plan never records one.
