## What the wider system shows

**Learning:** The management-of-change procedure existed at company level but was not in use, so part and material substitutions reached the work site with no review. [FND-F7FE8463; FND-3FEE59DF; FND-B86F67F0; EVD-2986]

Two company-level codes carry it down the map: OIC 2.1 links to routine violations at level 5 (5 mentions) and OIC 2.7 to misapplied procedures at level 5 (5 mentions). The procedure was published in 2016 and no change was put through it after 2018 [EVD-2986].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-06)*

**Learning:** No procedure for calibrating high-pressure meters and returning them to service existed at the operational management level, so the work at the meter had no checklist or test to follow. [FND-257C1100; FND-137BC719; FND-D58142C9; EVD-2894]

SVI 1.4 at level 4 links to checklists not followed at level 5 and to unclear technical guidance at level 6, 6 mentions each. The gasket was replaced and the meter returned to service with no test above operating pressure [EVD-2894].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-02)*

**Learning:** Safety audits at company level used generic checklists, so assurance could not see plant-specific conditions at the work site. [FND-C9C77FB7; FND-5BA62765; FND-B93AE333; EVD-7681]

OIC 2.7 at level 3 links to checklists not followed at level 5 (5 mentions), and supervisory oversight at level 4 (SVI 1.1) to the same code (6 mentions). The 2018 and 2019 internal audits kept no filled checklists [EVD-7681].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-20)*

**Learning:** Company practice for known problems shaped the equipment level: repeat failures of the same seal stayed routine repairs, not a reliability warning. [FND-82FC718D; FND-350C35AD; EVD-1494]

OIC 2.1 at level 3 and maintenance at level 6 (PTE 2.12) both link to routine violations at level 5 (5 and 4 mentions). The seal was replaced three times in 22 months [EVD-1494].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-05)*

**Learning:** The company's emergency plan named the major-leak scenario but gave no isolation criteria, and the gap carried through the operational guidance to the people responding. [FND-730D0DCA; FND-DF1144C3; FND-4F687CC5; EVD-4052]

OIC 2.1 and OIC 2.7 at level 3 each link to SVI 1.4 at level 4 (4 mentions each), and SVI 1.4 links to unclear technical guidance at level 6. The plan covered leaks but not isolation or stoppage [EVD-4052].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-07)*
