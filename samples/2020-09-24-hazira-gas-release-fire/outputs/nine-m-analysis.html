M1 Manpower/Mindpower

3

c-m1-1

A high-consequence seal job was staffed with no independent check role, so the part, the method and the missing test passed straight into service.

Subcategory M1.08 Communication and Coordination: nobody on the job was set up to check it and hand it over. The 18 Sep 2020 gasket job had no independent quality-check role, no hydrotest witness and no closing supervisor (OIS 4.2) []. System driver: the maintenance and spares programme (OIR 1.6, level 4) feeds the gap in enforcing existing rules (SVV 4.1), and no return-to-service procedure existed to staff the job against. Barrier state: absent.

+1 supporting

Independent check of pressure-boundary work before return to service

Control: administrativeState: absent

Failure mode

absent: no quality-check role, hydrotest witness or closing supervisor on the job

Language check JC6 accepted: the barrier records the administrative control the export shows was in place; the change for this condition is at the elimination tier

Corrective: remove the field reseal task from this class of joint, so that no crew has to judge the part, the method and the test alone. Pressure-boundary seals on the metering skid are restored by exchanging a shop-built assembly that has already passed its pressure test.

M2 Medium

3

c-m2-1

The vapour cloud made the leak area unreachable, and isolating the release depended on reaching it.

Subcategory M2.09 Environmental Stressors and Extreme Conditions. Dense vapour kept operating staff from approaching the metering area to find and isolate the source, a 41-minute delay before remote isolation (PTE 2.3) []. System driver: a culture that allowed the unsafe set-up (OIC 2.1, level 4) feeds a workspace that did not fit the operation (PTE 2.8): the emergency valves could only be worked by hand at the valve. Barrier state: degraded.

+1 supporting

Control: administrativeState: degraded

Failure mode

degraded: the vapour cloud blocked the approach

Language check JC6 accepted: the barrier records the administrative control the export shows was in place; the change for this condition is at the elimination tier

Preventive: remove the need for anyone to approach a release to isolate it. Every emergency isolation point for the metering area is worked from outside the area a release can cover.

Control: elimination

Leading indicatorEmergency isolation points on the skid that can be worked without entering the release zone

M3 Method

3

c-m3-1

A change and a high-risk job went ahead without the formal risk assessment the method required.

Subcategory M3.06 Risk Management Integration. The O-ring substitution never went through the management-of-change risk assessment, which had not been used for any change since 2018 []; the gasket replacement ran on a maintenance notification with no job safety analysis, permit or procedure []; and the emergency plan's risk assessment left out isolation criteria [] (SVIO 2.4, three findings). System driver: the maintenance and spares programme (OIR 1.6, level 4) feeds risks that were identified but not mitigated (SVF 3.4). Barrier state: absent.

Failure mode

Preventive: remove the seal that could be swapped without a risk assessment. Replace the O-ring-sealed joints on the metering pressure boundary with welded connections where the design allows, so no seal is left to substitute outside the management-of-change process.

The site's working doctrine made risky conditions normal: no isolation criteria in the emergency plan, breakdown repair for flameproof equipment, and unspecified seals as regular practice.

Subcategory M4.08 Organisational Culture and Mission Alignment, kept in M4 rather than M1 because it is what the organisation's doctrine made normal, not how a team was led day to day. The emergency plan named the major-leak scenario with no isolation criteria or authority []; breakdown repair was preferred over preventive inspection in Zone 1 []; and locally bought O-rings were 'regular practice' [] (OIP 3.5, three findings). System driver: formal risk assessment (SVIO 2.4, level 3) feeds mission planning (PTF 1.13). Barrier state: absent.

System driver: the maintenance and spares programme (OIR 1.6, level 4) feeds risks that were identified but not mitigated (SVF 3.4); pipe-wall monitoring had slipped off its cycle. Barrier state: absent.
