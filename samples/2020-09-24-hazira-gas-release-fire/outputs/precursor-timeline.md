## Bottom line

- The most repeated signal was the meter seal itself: the meter was serviced in November 2018 and resealed in February 2019 and again in September 2020 with locally bought O-rings, and the repeats were never escalated into a root-cause review. [FND-AC9A1E69; FND-D14B8424; EVD-1494; EVD-8645] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-05)*
- The last signals came days before the release: a malfunction notification on 17 September 2020 that was never closed, and a reseal on 18 September proved only at operating pressure. [FND-FA807C69; FND-FDFA6FF5; EVD-0591; EVD-2894] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-04)*
- The longest-running signal was pipe-wall loss: thickness surveys six years apart against a four-year schedule, a 2017 report asking for corrective action, and pitting in July 2019 that was painted. The findings do not link these lines to the failed meter. [FND-6FA2CEDE; FND-C6992EAF; FND-ABDADA0C; FND-98A4D631; EVD-8307; EVD-5527; EVD-2397] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-17)*
- The organisation had dated chances to learn: an external audit's open recommendations extended without approval, a repeat seal job, an unrecorded calibration change and a notification left open; none led to a change before the release. [FND-1D713648; FND-82FC718D; FND-E94D563C; FND-FA807C69; EVD-6852; EVD-1494; EVD-2506; EVD-0591] *(evidence: direct)*

## Signal learnings

**Learning:** Repeat repairs on the same item were not counted, so a failing seal looked like routine work each time. [FND-110F964C; FND-82FC718D; EVD-1494]

Three seal jobs on one meter in 22 months were each handled as a fresh repair with the same substitute part, and none was escalated for root cause [FND-110F964C; FND-82FC718D; EVD-1494]. Nothing in the maintenance system turned the repeat into a reliability warning.

- Owner: Mechanical Maintenance (F02, T3)

*(evidence: direct; learning: LR-05)*

**Indicator:** Repeat repairs reviewed [FND-110F964C]
- Measure: Number of components with a second corrective job within 12 months, from the maintenance notification history, and the share with a completed root-cause review.
- Deterioration looks like: A third repair of the same component with no review.
- Owner: Mechanical Maintenance (F02, T3)

**Learning:** Inspection results reached a report but not a corrective plan. [FND-C6992EAF; FND-ABDADA0C; EVD-5527; EVD-2397]

The 2017 thickness report asked for corrective action and no plan followed in three years [FND-C6992EAF; EVD-5527]; the July 2019 pitting was answered with painting only [FND-ABDADA0C; EVD-2397]. Measurement was treated as the action.

- Owner: Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection (F05, T3)

*(evidence: direct; learning: LR-17)*

**Indicator:** Inspection findings with a corrective plan [FND-C6992EAF]
- Measure: Share of inspection findings above the action threshold that have an approved corrective plan, and the age of those that do not, from the inspection and integrity action records.
- Deterioration looks like: Any wall-loss finding open without a fitness-for-service decision.
- Owner: Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection (F05, T3)

**Learning:** An inspection schedule that slipped had no escalation, so the slip itself sent no signal. [FND-6FA2CEDE; FND-C4B133AA; EVD-8307; EVD-8287]

The four-year thickness schedule ran six years between 2011 and 2017 with no supervisory enforcement, and baseline data was missing [FND-6FA2CEDE; FND-C4B133AA; EVD-8307; EVD-8287].

- Owner: Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection (F05, T2)

*(evidence: direct; learning: LR-18)*

**Indicator:** Overdue inspections [FND-6FA2CEDE]
- Measure: Number of scheduled thickness inspections past their due date, and the longest overrun, from the inspection schedule.
- Deterioration looks like: Any inspection overdue by more than one cycle.
- Owner: Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection (F05, T2)

**Learning:** A change that leaves no record cannot be seen by anyone after the job. [FND-E94D563C; FND-D58142C9; FND-5A5F6AB3; EVD-2506; EVD-2986]

The December 2019 calibration change and its reversal are not in the signed documents, and no checklist was used [FND-E94D563C; FND-D58142C9; EVD-2506]. The seal substitution was likewise never recorded as a change [FND-5A5F6AB3; EVD-2986].

- Owner: Instrumentation, Control & Calibration (F04, T2)

*(evidence: direct; learning: LR-16)*

**Indicator:** Calibrations with a complete change record [FND-E94D563C]
- Measure: Share of calibrations on safety-relevant instruments with a completed checklist recording as-found and as-left settings, checked by a second person.
- Deterioration looks like: Any signed calibration record that does not list a setting change found later.
- Owner: Instrumentation, Control & Calibration (F04, T2)

**Learning:** A notification left open after the job closes the loop on paper only. [FND-FA807C69; FND-646BAC1F; EVD-0591]

The malfunction notification of 17 September 2020 was never closed or reviewed after the meter went back into service, so the close-out review that could have caught the missing test did not happen [FND-FA807C69; FND-646BAC1F; EVD-0591].
