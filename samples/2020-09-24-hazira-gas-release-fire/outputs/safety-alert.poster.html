Safety Alert: Hazira gas terminal condensate release and fire, 2020

Bottom line

  • A seal that does not match the original specification is a change, not a spare.
  • An opened pressure joint is not proven until it passes a recorded test to design pressure.
  • Work that can cause a major release needs a check by someone other than the person doing it.

If the specification for a pressure-boundary part is lost and no role owns it, crews fit whatever part is to hand, and the substitute becomes normal practice.

If no procedure says how an opened high-pressure joint is tested before service, crews use the quickest check, and a weak joint passes.

If a job that can cause a major release is planned like routine work, the fitter's knowledge is the only barrier.

Do not return an opened pressure-boundary joint to service until a recorded test to design pressure has passed.

List the pressure-boundary seals on legacy equipment that have no specification on file.

Ask stores whether a pressure-boundary seal can be issued without a specification reference.
