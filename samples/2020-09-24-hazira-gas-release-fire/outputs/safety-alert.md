## Bottom line

- A seal that does not match the original specification is a change, not a spare. [FND-8C0BB623; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-01)*
- An opened pressure joint is not proven until it passes a recorded test to design pressure. [FND-212DC9B7; EVD-2894] *(learning: LR-02)*
- Work that can cause a major release needs a check by someone other than the person doing it. [FND-A6312B6B; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-03)*

## What happened

On 24 September 2020 condensate vapour escaped from a seal on a 1986 metering skid at a gas terminal, six days after the seal had been replaced [FND-8C0BB623; EVD-8645]. The vapour cloud spread for about 46 minutes before the first isolation valve was closed by hand [FND-322112EE; EVD-6414]. It then ignited at a damaged flameproof light fitting and caused a major fire [FND-CCE6F868; EVD-6916].

## Why it matters to you

All three learnings apply industry-wide. Any site with legacy pressure equipment, locally bought spares or maintenance run on a work notification alone can hold the same conditions today [FND-2FAB8D51; FND-7C5ED5B3; EVD-8645].

## What we learned

**Learning:** If the specification for a pressure-boundary part is lost and no role owns it, crews fit whatever part is to hand, and the substitute becomes normal practice. [FND-8C0BB623; FND-7A32AC87; FND-89962239; FND-B356F15B; FND-91B45FF9; FND-DA67926D; FND-54180594; FND-82673283; FND-45363DCA; FND-A2048FB8; FND-FFE174D0; FND-A443D288; FND-72A7D26C; FND-CEE4B00B; FND-3746C7B6; FND-42BEF461; FND-D66ACA7B; FND-852B7EC7; FND-DEAA7F90; FND-A3D7D53F; FND-B86F67F0; FND-D40839C1; FND-BC4FD086; FND-23139BF9; FND-EBB66554; FND-B440C1BF; FND-8CE00707; FND-6B3A6492; FND-A0017E28; FND-B56701CA; FND-2B0A49CB; FND-35861FFB; FND-2CEE8915; FND-48C48354; FND-9A8F3D7A; FND-7832DDF6; FND-DE30BD8B; EVD-8645; EVD-1494; EVD-0064]

Locally bought O-rings replaced a slot gasket with no specification, check or approval; the seal fitted on 18 September failed six days later.

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-01)*

**Learning:** If no procedure says how an opened high-pressure joint is tested before service, crews use the quickest check, and a weak joint passes. [FND-7C5ED5B3; FND-FD617187; FND-212DC9B7; FND-15279F97; FND-7FA02383; FND-4944554C; FND-FDFA6FF5; FND-1662466D; FND-9174A5B0; FND-611A8BFC; FND-904FCA63; FND-C34A026C; FND-257C1100; FND-137BC719; FND-8ACDE078; FND-5919B328; FND-C4E06264; FND-3AB996FC; FND-86CB3683; FND-491EDE74; FND-50C7319E; FND-B91D4A23; FND-7A10FF70; FND-71EE9734; EVD-2894; EVD-0185]

The joint was pressurised only to operating pressure, with no test to design pressure, and went back online about 46 hours later.

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-02)*

**Learning:** If a job that can cause a major release is planned like routine work, the fitter's knowledge is the only barrier. [FND-2FAB8D51; FND-A6312B6B; FND-28F8790B; FND-59A2F497; FND-F8C125D5; FND-615DB819; FND-7DF14ADB; FND-31459031; FND-63B6D260; EVD-8645; EVD-2894; EVD-5105]

No one was assigned to check the part, the method or the test.

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-03)*

## What to check now

- [ ] **Hold point:** Do not return an opened pressure-boundary joint to service until a recorded test to design pressure has passed. [FND-212DC9B7; EVD-2894] *(learning: LR-02)*
- [ ] List the pressure-boundary seals on legacy equipment that have no specification on file. [FND-8C0BB623; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-01)*
- [ ] Ask stores whether a pressure-boundary seal can be issued without a specification reference. [FND-2FAB8D51; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-01)*
- [ ] Check whether safety-critical maintenance jobs carry an independent check of part, method and test. [FND-A6312B6B; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-03)*

## Questions for your crew

**Q: When you change a seal, how do you know the new one matches the original specification?** [FND-8C0BB623; EVD-8645]
- Why it matters: Shows whether the specification is on file or held only in people's memory.

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Q: What test does a joint you opened pass before it goes back into service, and who sees the result?** [FND-212DC9B7; FND-A6312B6B; EVD-2894]
- Why it matters: Shows whether the test is written down and checked by someone other than the fitter.

*(learning: LR-02)*
