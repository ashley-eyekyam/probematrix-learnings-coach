## Directions for stronger change

**Recommendation:** Make the four-year thickness cycle a condition the maintenance system enforces: an inspection on a pressure-boundary line cannot be moved past its due point without the integrity function's recorded assessment of that line. [FND-75CEBE09; FND-5ABEE30B; FND-172D2FE4; EVD-8307; EVD-8287]
- Control tier: engineering
- Leading indicator: Thickness inspections on pressure-boundary lines done within the four-year cycle, as a share of those due

*(learning: LR-18)*

**Recommendation:** Take an unverified drawing out of isolation planning: isolation and work-planning screens draw their valve and meter lists from a field-verified, controlled P&ID, and a drawing with no verification record cannot be issued for that use. [FND-72DCEE68; FND-D25BF914; FND-583EB5FE; EVD-0933]
- Control tier: engineering
- Leading indicator: Isolation plans for the metering system drawn from a field-verified, controlled P&ID, as a share of all such plans

*(learning: LR-19)*

**Recommendation:** Make the drill record unable to close without what the drill showed: the drill report cannot be saved as complete until its detection, notification and isolation times and its observations are entered, and each action it raises opens a tracked item. [FND-DC0751CF; FND-B8B75D91; EVD-2130]
- Control tier: engineering
- Leading indicator: Drill records closed with timings and observations entered, as a share of all drills

*(learning: LR-24)*

**Recommendation:** Question the assumption the audit programme rests on, that a completed checklist shows a barrier works: each audit samples named safety-critical barriers on the plant and records a reading, test or inspection result for each one. [FND-4215704E; FND-C0466EB3; FND-303495E9; EVD-9692]
- Control tier: administrative
- Leading indicator: Audit items on safety-critical barriers backed by a recorded reading, test or inspection result
- Why not stronger: Assurance is a management activity: no engineered control replaces looking at the plant. The register's change already moves the audit towards site evidence; this direction questions what the audit counts as proof.

*(learning: LR-20)*

**Recommendation:** Question the norm that a repeat repair closes the job: a second failure of the same pressure-boundary item within a year is reviewed as a sign that the design or the part is wrong, and the review asks why the earlier repairs were accepted as enough. [FND-8DA35CA9; FND-69067A1C; FND-71D631C1; EVD-1494]
- Control tier: administrative
- Leading indicator: Repeat failures on pressure-boundary items reviewed for design or part fitness, as a share of all repeat failures
- Why not stronger: The engineering change, an automatic repeat-failure trigger, is already in the register; this direction questions the rule that let three repairs count as enough.

*(learning: LR-05)*

*Language check JC6 accepted: a double-loop direction that questions the rule behind the cause; the engineering change for this learning is already in the register*

**Recommendation:** Revisit the design basis that let protective systems inform rather than act: list the site's other detection and alarm functions that only display, and record for each whether an automatic action is needed, and the reason when it stays display-only. [FND-A245072B; FND-94C28ACE; FND-6155A1C2; EVD-8657; EVD-3452]
- Control tier: administrative
- Leading indicator: Display-only detection and alarm functions with a recorded decision on automatic action
- Why not stronger: The engineering change for the metering and Gas Terminal areas is already in the register; this direction asks the same question of the rest of the site.

*(learning: LR-10)*

*Language check JC6 accepted: a double-loop direction that questions the rule behind the cause; the engineering change for this learning is already in the register*

[FND-98A4D631; FND-5EC15274; FND-47B1702F; EVD-5527; EVD-2397]
