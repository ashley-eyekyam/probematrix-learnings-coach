## Bottom line

- The seal that leaked was a locally bought part fitted as regular practice, because the original specification was lost and no one owned it. [FND-B86F67F0; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-01)*
- After the repair, the meter was pressurised to operating pressure only and went back online the next day; no procedure asked for more. [FND-491EDE74; EVD-2894] *(learning: LR-02)*
- The night shift had no written rule on when to isolate and no authority to do it without calling up the chain, and the valves could only be closed by hand inside the cloud. [FND-82761CC5; FND-7D28BE30; EVD-4052; EVD-3932] *(learning: LR-07)*
- A flameproof light fitting with three missing bolts was the probable ignition source; electrical fittings were repaired on breakdown, not inspected. [FND-675D99DD; FND-5E08D0F8; EVD-6916] *(learning: LR-11)*
- Most of what the analysis found sits with supervision and the organisation, not with the people doing the work. [FND-23139BF9; FND-F7FE8463]

## What happened

In the early hours of 24 September 2020, condensate vapour escaped from a seal on an old metering skid at the gas terminal. Security staff on the perimeter heard the noise and saw the cloud first, and they phoned it in through the CCTV room. The alarm in the gas terminal control room came after that [FND-439E0DF7; FND-95F9205E; FND-9399FA02; EVD-4286; EVD-6234; EVD-8657].

Control-room staff went out to find the leak, but the cloud was so dense that nobody could get near it. The emergency valves could only be closed by hand at the valve, and the plan did not say when the shift could isolate on its own. The decision went up the chain by phone, and the first valve was closed about 46 minutes after the leak was first seen [FND-BDD73BA2; FND-82761CC5; FND-AE629313; EVD-9220; EVD-6414].

A few minutes after the valves were closed, the cloud reached a damaged flameproof light fitting and caught fire. The seal had been replaced on 18 September with a locally bought O-ring, the same kind of part used in earlier repairs [FND-675D99DD; FND-74A80D24; FND-BC4FD086; FND-7A32AC87; EVD-4507; EVD-2894; EVD-1494].

## What the investigation says about the system

**Learning:** If you cannot confirm a seal or gasket matches the specification for a pressure part, stop and ask for the spec. Do not fit 'what we normally use'. [FND-8C0BB623; FND-7A32AC87; FND-89962239; FND-B356F15B; FND-91B45FF9; FND-DA67926D; FND-54180594; FND-82673283; FND-45363DCA; FND-A2048FB8; FND-FFE174D0; FND-A443D288; FND-72A7D26C; FND-CEE4B00B; FND-3746C7B6; FND-42BEF461; FND-D66ACA7B; FND-852B7EC7; FND-DEAA7F90; FND-A3D7D53F; FND-B86F67F0; FND-D40839C1; FND-BC4FD086; FND-23139BF9; FND-EBB66554; FND-B440C1BF; FND-8CE00707; FND-6B3A6492; FND-A0017E28; FND-B56701CA; FND-2B0A49CB; FND-35861FFB; FND-2CEE8915; FND-48C48354; FND-9A8F3D7A; FND-7832DDF6; FND-DE30BD8B; EVD-8645; EVD-1494; EVD-0064]

If the original specification for a pressure-boundary part is lost and no role owns re-establishing it, maintainers will fit whatever part is to hand, and the substitute becomes normal practice, because the job has to be done and nothing in the system says 'stop'.

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Learning:** After any work that opens a pressure boundary, ask: what test proves this joint, and who witnessed it? Write the answer on the job record. [FND-7C5ED5B3; FND-FD617187; FND-212DC9B7; FND-15279F97; FND-7FA02383; FND-4944554C; FND-FDFA6FF5; FND-1662466D; FND-9174A5B0; FND-611A8BFC; FND-904FCA63; FND-C34A026C; FND-257C1100; FND-137BC719; FND-8ACDE078; FND-5919B328; FND-C4E06264; FND-3AB996FC; FND-86CB3683; FND-491EDE74; FND-50C7319E; FND-B91D4A23; FND-7A10FF70; FND-71EE9734; EVD-2894; EVD-0185]

If no procedure defines how a high-pressure joint is tested before return to service, crews will use the quickest available check (pressurising to operating pressure), and a weak joint will pass, because operating pressure does not prove the joint against the pressures it must withstand.

*(learning: LR-02)*

**Learning:** On a confirmed major hydrocarbon release, isolation is your first job. You are authorised to do it; report while you act. [FND-314D34C5; FND-DE132C95; FND-AE629313; FND-81E3EEA0; FND-500E0B2D; FND-8DAFDADC; FND-7505B1AF; FND-2EC435D0; FND-BB4CC662; FND-D4A3EF8A; FND-4F687CC5; FND-D1DF4895; FND-7A2429CE; FND-33D32D4C; FND-DF1144C3; FND-82761CC5; FND-730D0DCA; FND-6472CD15; FND-E009D26B; FND-5C7B163D; FND-446A7956; FND-D4A6F6B6; FND-A206DFD6; FND-8A8F94A5; EVD-4052; EVD-6414; EVD-3932; EVD-9612; EVD-7340]

If an emergency plan names a release scenario but not who may isolate, and on what criteria, isolation waits for escalation up the chain, because people at the scene reasonably believe they need permission to shut in a major supply.

*(learning: LR-07)*

**Learning:** A flameproof fitting with a missing bolt or open cover is not flameproof. Report it and make it safe the same shift. [FND-784A23E4; FND-74A80D24; FND-C7F16268; FND-554BFB4A; FND-1D61DC3A; FND-ADC25F29; FND-0B35D995; FND-5E08D0F8; FND-675D99DD; FND-1280E788; FND-ABF11182; FND-CCE6F868; FND-76BE46EF; FND-E6759E06; FND-D1709E71; FND-198069BD; FND-9066FB74; FND-5C43E7A3; FND-FD533742; FND-02C51A67; FND-B1D1AAC1; FND-30EC643B; EVD-6916; EVD-9999; EVD-0496]

If electrical maintenance in hazardous areas responds to breakdowns instead of inspecting explosion-protected equipment, defects that do not stop the equipment working (missing bolts, open enclosures) stay in service until a release finds them.

*(learning: LR-11)*

**Learning:** Ask for practice on the emergencies you are expected to handle. If you have never practised one, tell your supervisor. [FND-337CB67F; FND-E4596837; FND-4C146B0E; FND-02B682F3; FND-D2343F46; FND-4B285961; FND-7885BCF5; FND-F2D32B1D; FND-E3D07562; FND-FA1C9404; FND-78ED8552; FND-8EC441F3; FND-0AEE9898; FND-64E24A9E; FND-2AA87E93; FND-E44E0EDA; FND-42043FE6; FND-F6B552F6; FND-268CA069; FND-6CBACB54; FND-14D093BD; FND-47E58DF8; FND-057E89A7; FND-954553FD; EVD-5634; EVD-1460; EVD-9220]

If no qualification is defined for control-room operators, people are assigned to emergency duty after general training only, and a rare, time-critical event becomes their first practice, because rostering fills posts with whoever is available.

*(learning: LR-12)*

The analysis places most causes at supervision and organisational level: 71% of the findings sit at levels 3 and 4. The conditions the crews worked in (a lost specification, no test procedure, an emergency plan without isolation rules, valves that could not be closed from a safe place) were set by the organisation [FND-23139BF9; FND-B91D4A23; FND-DF1144C3; FND-7D28BE30].

## Questions to raise with management

**Q: Which pressure seals and gaskets on our units have no specification on file, and what stops a crew fitting 'what we normally use'?** [FND-B86F67F0; FND-B56701CA; EVD-8645]
- Why it matters: The leaking seal was a local part fitted as regular practice for lack of a specification.
- Put to: Maintenance manager

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Q: What test is now required before pressure-boundary work goes back into service, and who witnesses it?** [FND-491EDE74; FND-B91D4A23; EVD-2894]
- Why it matters: The meter went back online after an operating-pressure soak, with no procedure for a design-pressure test.
- Put to: Maintenance manager

*(learning: LR-02)*

**Q: Who on the night shift may isolate a major release without phoning for permission, and is that written down?** [FND-82761CC5; EVD-3932]
- Why it matters: No role below the plant manager had standing authority to close the isolation valve.
- Put to: Operations manager

*(learning: LR-07)*

**Q: Which emergency valves on our units can only be closed by hand at the valve, and what is the plan for them?** [FND-7D28BE30; EVD-3932]
- Why it matters: The valves here had no remote operation, and the cloud kept people away from them.
- Put to: Operations manager

*(learning: LR-09)*

**Q: What qualification and emergency practice does someone need before they work alone in a control room?** [FND-337CB67F; EVD-5634]
- Why it matters: Control-room staff had a four-day general course, no defined qualification and no simulator practice.
- Put to: Operations manager

*(learning: LR-12)*

**Q: When will flameproof fittings in zoned areas be inspected on a plan, and how do crews report a damaged one?** [FND-5E08D0F8; FND-675D99DD; EVD-6916]
- Why it matters: A fitting with three missing bolts stayed in service and was the probable ignition source.
- Put to: Electrical maintenance manager

*(learning: LR-11)*

**Q: Who may stop a job when the permit or job safety plan is missing, and are missing permits now tracked?** [FND-7E1F2B3F; EVD-5105]
- Why it matters: Some work permits were missing with no tracking, and job safety plans were absent.
- Put to: HSE manager

*(learning: LR-22)*

**Q: When a crew fixes the same fault again, what happens to that report, and who reviews it?** [FND-82FC718D; EVD-1494]
- Why it matters: The same seal was changed three times in about 22 months with no root-cause review.
- Put to: Maintenance manager

*(learning: LR-05)*

**Q: How are concerns raised by crews recorded, answered and reported back to the committee?** [FND-A443D288; FND-3A34BAD3]
- Why it matters: The repeat seal changes and the substitute part were known on site, but nothing recorded shows they reached a decision.
- Put to: Site leadership

## What to look for on site

- [ ] Seals, gaskets or other pressure parts kept in stores without a specification or certificate. [FND-EBB66554; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-01)*
- [ ] Pressure-boundary jobs closed with no test record or witness signature. [FND-491EDE74; EVD-2894] *(learning: LR-02)*
- [ ] Maintenance notifications left open on equipment that is back in service. [FND-267F1E09; EVD-0591] *(learning: LR-04)*
- [ ] Flameproof fittings in zoned areas with missing bolts, open covers or damaged glands. [FND-675D99DD; EVD-6916] *(learning: LR-11)*
- [ ] Emergency isolation valves that can only be operated by hand in an area a release could fill. [FND-7D28BE30; EVD-3932] *(learning: LR-09)*
- [ ] Work under way without a permit or job safety plan at the job. [FND-7E1F2B3F; EVD-5105] *(learning: LR-22)*
- [ ] Drawings at the job that do not match the valves and meters in the field. [FND-583EB5FE; EVD-0933] *(learning: LR-19)*
- [ ] Fire-water pumps under maintenance or tripping on test with no recorded reason. [FND-D8BC7096; EVD-2432] *(learning: LR-23)*

## Running the discussion

Ground rules: no names, no blame, and the discussion stays on conditions, not on people. The aim is to find what made the work hard and what the organisation is changing. Remind the committee that this guide is a draft from an analysis no person has finished reviewing [FND-23139BF9; FND-B91D4A23].

Anonymity: offer an anonymous route for concerns and record each concern by topic, not by who raised it. Do not describe a person so that the crew can tell who it is; a small crew's single operator is identifiable by role alone. Minutes and the concerns log go to management without names [FND-A443D288; FND-71EE9734].
