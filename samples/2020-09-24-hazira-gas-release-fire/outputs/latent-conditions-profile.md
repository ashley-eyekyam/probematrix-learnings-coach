## Latent conditions

Each condition runs under several separate findings and register learnings. The counts show how widely the investigation found it, not how much it contributed.

**Learning:** High-consequence work went ahead without written guidance, because no one had written the specification, procedure or criteria the work needed. [FND-DE30BD8B; FND-257C1100; FND-4D7CF773; FND-D4A3EF8A; EVD-8645; EVD-2894; EVD-8657; EVD-4052]

Missing policy and guidance from supervision (SVI 1.4) recurs in 21 findings, more than any other code. The gaps include the substitute gasket specification [EVD-8645], a return-to-service procedure for the high-pressure meter [EVD-2894], automatic shutdown from gas detection [EVD-8657] and the emergency plan's isolation criteria [EVD-4052]. The same code sits under 12 register learnings, including LR-01, LR-02 and LR-07.

*(evidence: corroborated; +5 supporting)*

**Learning:** Risks the site knew about were accepted as normal working, so unsafe conditions became the way tasks were done. [FND-B86F67F0; FND-119E6CF7; FND-7D28BE30; FND-5E08D0F8; EVD-8645; EVD-3452; EVD-3932; EVD-6916]

A culture that allowed unsafe tasks (OIC 2.1) has 36 links out, the most of any code, and appears in 14 of the 25 register learnings. It covers locally bought O-rings as a regular practice [EVD-8645], gas detection that only displayed an alarm [EVD-3452], isolation valves with no remote operation [EVD-3932] and a damaged flameproof lamp left in service [EVD-6916].

*(evidence: corroborated; +4 supporting)*

**Learning:** Repeat failures of the same seal were handled as routine repairs, so the warning they gave never reached a reliability review. [FND-3A34BAD3; FND-1A251687; FND-82FC718D; FND-71D631C1; EVD-1494; EVD-0591]

Inconsistent risk management (OIC 2.7) recurs in 19 findings and has 24 links out; the maintenance, spares and support programme (OIR 1.6) has 26 links out. Both meet at meter 310FT01: three gasket replacements in about 22 months were not escalated for root-cause action [EVD-1494] [EVD-0591].

*(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-05)*

**Learning:** The change procedure existed on paper but was not in use, so a material substitution on a pressure boundary had no change review. [FND-F7FE8463; FND-6ECCE231; FND-3FEE59DF; FND-ADF96860; EVD-2986; EVD-8645]

The 2016 management-of-change procedure had not been used since 2018 [EVD-2986]. The same condition is coded four ways: missing guidance (SVI 1.4), a culture that allowed unsafe tasks (OIC 2.1), inconsistent risk management (OIC 2.7) and a problem left uncorrected (SVF 3.2).

*(evidence: corroborated; +2 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

**Learning:** Integrity findings that were measured and reported stayed open for years without a corrective plan. [FND-47B1702F; FND-C6992EAF; FND-E5C4209D; FND-0F19C9FC; EVD-5527; EVD-2397]

The Failure to Correct Known Problem row holds 40 findings; operations management (SVF 3.2) recurs 18 times and ineffective action against an identified risk (SVF 3.4) 14 times.
