  regular practice, because no role held or owned the specification.
  [FND-23139BF9; FND-8CE00707; EVD-8645]
- Written controls existed on paper but were not working: the
  management-of-change procedure had not been used since 2018, and the

  [FND-F7FE8463; FND-5C7B163D; EVD-2986; EVD-4052]

Ask of the board
----------------
To note: the investigation places most causes in the management system
(supervision, policy, resources and culture) rather than at the point of work
[FND-F7FE8463; FND-23139BF9; FND-B440C1BF].
To request assurance: that management confirms, with sampled evidence from
every site, the state of the controls listed under 'Assurance the board should
request' below [FND-1D713648; FND-303495E9; EVD-6852].

What happened and why it matters to the board
---------------------------------------------
In the early hours of 24 September 2020, condensate vapour escaped from a
flange seal on a 1986 metering skid at the gas terminal. The vapour cloud was
seen by perimeter security before the detection system alarmed, and the first
emergency isolation valve was closed about 46 minutes after detection. The
cloud ignited at 03:08:15, and a damaged flameproof light fitting was the
probable ignition source [FND-044D1DEC; FND-CCE6F868; FND-01C3DE98;
FND-D1DF4895; EVD-1284; EVD-6414; EVD-6916].
For the board, the event is a test of oversight more than an equipment
failure. The conditions behind it (lost part specifications, unused change
control, an emergency plan without isolation criteria, detection that only
displayed alarms) were in place for months or years, and the findings record
that none had been assessed as a risk needing a decision [FND-B440C1BF;
FND-2D90823E; FND-05841EDA].

What the investigation says about the management system
-------------------------------------------------------

* Assurance that did not detect
The self-safety audit checklist was not specific to the plant and recorded
compliance where the data was later found incomplete. The 2018 and 2019
internal audits kept no filled checklist, so their coverage cannot be checked.
Audit results looked reassuring while plant-specific defects stayed in place
[FND-303495E9; FND-48967DB2; FND-C0466EB3; EVD-9692; EVD-7681].

* Signals that did not escalate
The same meter seal was replaced three times in about 22 months, and a 2017
thickness survey found pipes needing corrective action with no plan three
years later. Both were recorded, but each was handled as routine work inside a
function instead of as a risk for a management decision [FND-1A251687;
FND-82FC718D; FND-47B1702F; EVD-1494; EVD-5527].

* Risk accepted without documented authority
Three kinds of risk were accepted with no recorded decision: the switch from
the original gasket to a locally bought O-ring, changes made outside the
management-of-change procedure since 2018, and twelve external-audit
recommendations whose target dates were extended without the approval the
procedure required [FND-BC4FD086; FND-F7FE8463; FND-1D713648; EVD-8645;
EVD-2986; EVD-6852].

* Design and resources
The fire and gas system was set up to display alarms only, with no automatic
shutdown or suppression, and the emergency isolation valves had no remote
operation from the control room. Isolation therefore depended on people
walking into the release area, and the emergency plan gave them no criteria or
delegated authority to isolate [FND-119E6CF7; FND-322112EE; FND-82761CC5;
EVD-3932; EVD-4052].

* Competence
Control-room staff took on emergency duties after a four-day general course,
with no qualification defined in the HR or operations policy and no simulator
practice [FND-337CB67F; EVD-5634].

Questions for directors to ask management
-----------------------------------------

* Which pressure-boundary parts across our sites have no specification on
  file, and who owns re-establishing them?
Put to: Executive management
Why it matters: The gasket specification was lost and no role owned it, so a
  substitute part became normal practice.
Good evidence looks like: A register of critical pressure-boundary spares by
  site, showing the specification and certificate on file and the function
  that owns each.
Sources: FND-23139BF9; FND-B56701CA; EVD-8645


* How many changes has each site put through the management-of-change
  procedure in the last year?
Put to: Executive management
Why it matters: The procedure existed from 2016 but no change had gone through
  it since 2018.
Good evidence looks like: A count of MOC records by site and period, with a
  sample traced from a work order to its MOC record.
Sources: FND-F7FE8463; FND-8083ECAC; EVD-2986


* Were earlier warnings acted on: the repeat seal replacements, the 2017
  thickness findings and the 2018 external-audit recommendations?
Put to: Executive management
Why it matters: Each was on record before the event, and none led to a
  decision that changed the plant.
Good evidence looks like: For each warning: when it was raised, who decided
  what, and the evidence that the action closed.
Sources: FND-1A251687; FND-47B1702F; FND-1D713648; EVD-1494; EVD-5527;
EVD-6852


* Who approved each extension of an overdue audit recommendation, and is that
  approval on file?
Put to: Executive management
Why it matters: Twelve of 308 recommendations from the February 2018 external
  audit were extended without the document showing the approval the procedure
  required.
Good evidence looks like: A list of extended recommendations with the
  approving authority and the record of each approval.
Sources: FND-1D713648; FND-B2AC0F8C; EVD-6852


* Do our safety audits test plant-specific evidence, or do they record
  compliance against generic checklists?
Put to: HSE and process safety function
Why it matters: Audits here recorded compliance without site data and could
  not find plant-specific defects.
Good evidence looks like: Audit checklists written for each plant, filled
  checklists retained, and a sample of 'compliant' findings re-tested by an
  independent reviewer.
Sources: FND-303495E9; FND-C0466EB3; EVD-9692; EVD-7681


* At which sites does emergency isolation depend on a person reaching a valve
  inside the hazard area?
Put to: Engineering function
Why it matters: The isolation valves here were local-manual only, and the
  vapour cloud kept people from reaching them.
Good evidence looks like: A site-by-site list of emergency isolation valves
  with their mode of operation, and the decisions taken on those that need
  local access.
Sources: FND-322112EE; FND-02310148; EVD-3932


* Which of our gas detection systems only raise an alarm, and what is the
  agreed response time for each?
Put to: Engineering function
Why it matters: Detection here only displayed an alarm, and it alarmed after
  people had already seen the cloud.
Good evidence looks like: A list of fire and gas systems by site, with their
  executive actions and the result of the last functional test.
Sources: FND-4458E0C0; FND-01C3DE98; EVD-8657


* Does every emergency plan state when to isolate and who may decide it on a
  night shift?
Put to: Site leadership
Why it matters: The plan named the major-leak scenario but gave no isolation
  criteria or delegated authority.
Good evidence looks like: Emergency plans with written isolation criteria and
  named delegated roles, and drill records showing those roles exercised.
Sources: FND-82761CC5; FND-DF1144C3; EVD-4052; EVD-3932


* How do we know control-room staff are qualified for emergency duties before
  they are rostered?
Put to: Site leadership
Why it matters: Staff here took on emergency duties after a four-day general
  course, with no defined qualification.
Good evidence looks like: A defined qualification for control-room emergency
  duties and the share of each shift that holds it.
Sources: FND-337CB67F; EVD-5634


Assurance the board should request
----------------------------------
  - Verification | Sites | Requested reporting date
  - Critical pressure-boundary spares have a specification and an
    approved-substitute record | All sites with legacy pressure equipment |
    For the board to set
  - Management-of-change records match the changes made in the field | All
    sites | For the board to set
  - Emergency plans state isolation criteria and delegated authority, and
    drills exercise them | All major-hazard sites | For the board to set
  - Emergency isolation valves and gas detection: mode of operation and
    executive actions | All major-hazard sites | For the board to set
  - Overdue and extended audit recommendations, with the approval for each
    extension | All sites | For the board to set
  - Independent re-test of a sample of 'compliant' safety-audit findings | All
    sites | For the board to set


Indicators for the board dashboard
----------------------------------

* Critical spares with a specification
Measure: Share of critical pressure-boundary spares with a specification and
  certificate on file, by site
Deterioration looks like: Any fall from the previous report, or any critical
  spare issued without a specification
Sources: FND-23139BF9; EVD-8645


* Change control in use
Measure: Number of MOC records opened and closed per site per quarter, with a
  sampled check of field changes against them
Deterioration looks like: A site reporting no MOC records in a quarter, or a
  field change found with no record
Sources: FND-F7FE8463; EVD-2986


* Overdue safety actions
Measure: Overdue audit recommendations and integrity actions, and the number
  extended without recorded approval
Deterioration looks like: Any extension without recorded approval, or a rising
  count of overdue actions
Sources: FND-1D713648; FND-47B1702F; EVD-6852; EVD-5527


* Repeat failures escalated
Measure: Repeat failures on the same safety-critical item that triggered an
  engineering review, as a share of all repeat failures
Deterioration looks like: Any repeat failure closed as routine repair without
  a review
Sources: FND-82FC718D; EVD-1494


* Time to emergency isolation in drills
Measure: Time from a confirmed release to first isolation in drills, by site
Deterioration looks like: A drill in which isolation waits for authority from
  above the shift
Sources: FND-82761CC5; FND-D1DF4895; EVD-6414
