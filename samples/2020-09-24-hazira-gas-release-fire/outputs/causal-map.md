## Bottom line

- Two organisational hubs feed the most links: a culture that allowed unsafe tasks (OIC 2.1, 36 links out) and a maintenance, spares and support programme that did not hold (OIR 1.6, 26 links out). Links out are a count of links, not a measure of strength. [FND-B86F67F0; FND-1A251687; FND-50C7319E] *(evidence: direct)*
- The pathway that matters most runs from the seal specification to the leak: no one owned the specification, supervision had no rule to enforce, the job had no guidance, and the joint went back into service on an unspecified O-ring without a test to design pressure. [FND-23139BF9; FND-852B7EC7; FND-82673283; FND-4944554C; EVD-8645; EVD-2894] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-01)*
- Inconsistent risk management of known problems (OIC 2.7) links to supervision not responding to critical information (SVI 1.6): repeated seal failures and an open notification did not start a root-cause review. [FND-3A34BAD3; FND-110F964C; FND-FA807C69; EVD-1494; EVD-0591] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-05)*
- After the release, the chain runs through command and control resources (OIR 1.1) that did not support isolation: valves that could be closed only by hand at the valve, gas detection that alarmed without acting, and an emergency plan with no isolation criteria. [FND-044D1DEC; FND-6155A1C2; FND-446A7956; EVD-3932; EVD-3452; EVD-4052] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-09)*

## Causal pathways

Each pathway reads top-down, one sentence per level, and ends on the condition at the sharp end.

### Seal specification to leak

No role owned the meter gasket specification or the authority to approve a substitute, and the original specification was not on file (L4) [FND-23139BF9; FND-B56701CA; EVD-8645]. Supervision had no specification, procurement or quality-control rule to enforce, so the locally bought O-ring became the regular repair part (L3) [FND-DE30BD8B; FND-852B7EC7]. At the job, the specification and a quality check for the part were not available (L2) [FND-82673283]. The 1986 flange, machined for a rectangular-slot gasket, was resealed on 18 September 2020 with an unspecified round O-ring (L1) [FND-6B3A6492; EVD-0064]. [FND-23139BF9; FND-B56701CA; EVD-8645; FND-DE30BD8B; FND-852B7EC7; FND-82673283; FND-6B3A6492; EVD-0064] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-01)*

### Return to service without a test

No procedure set how high-pressure meters were tested and returned to service after maintenance (L4) [FND-50C7319E; EVD-2894]. Pressurising to operating pressure had become the modelled norm, and the missing hydrostatic test was not picked up before the meter went back online (L3) [FND-71EE9734; FND-8ACDE078]. The repaired joint returned to service with a substitute seal and no hydrotest (L2) [FND-1662466D]. The joint was proved only at operating pressure and was in use when it failed (L1) [FND-4944554C; EVD-0185]. [FND-50C7319E; EVD-2894; FND-71EE9734; FND-8ACDE078; FND-1662466D; FND-4944554C; EVD-0185] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-02)*

### Repeat failures read as routine

Recurring gasket failures on the meter were treated as routine maintenance, and the risk of the pattern was never assessed (L4) [FND-3A34BAD3; FND-E0AE86A6; EVD-1494]. Supervision had no trigger to turn three gasket replacements in 22 months into a root-cause investigation (L3) [FND-110F964C; FND-AC9A1E69]. The chronically failing meter stayed in service across the replacements (L2) [FND-69067A1C]. Each failure was handled as a fresh repair with the same substitute part, so the pattern produced no change (L1) [FND-8DA35CA9]. [FND-3A34BAD3; FND-E0AE86A6; EVD-1494; FND-110F964C; FND-AC9A1E69; FND-69067A1C; FND-8DA35CA9] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-05)*

### Isolation waiting for authority

The on-site disaster management plan covered hydrocarbon leakage but gave no isolation or stoppage criteria and no delegated authority to isolate (L4) [FND-82761CC5; EVD-4052]. With no standard criteria, each responder improvised and the decision travelled up the night-shift chain (L3) [FND-D1DF4895; EVD-3932]. A steep authority gradient left people at the scene waiting for direction (L2) [FND-7505B1AF; EVD-7340]. The first emergency isolation valve was closed 46 minutes after the leak was first detected (L1) [FND-AEA16F83; EVD-6414]. [FND-82761CC5; EVD-4052; FND-D1DF4895; EVD-3932; FND-7505B1AF; EVD-7340; FND-AEA16F83; EVD-6414] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-07)*

### Valves out of reach

The emergency isolation valves were bought and kept as local-manual valves, never upgraded for remote operation from the control room (L4) [FND-044D1DEC; FND-02310148; EVD-3932]. That design was left uncorrected (L3) [FND-DBE08E73]. Dense vapour around the leak stopped anyone approaching the source or the valves (L2) [FND-BDD73BA2; EVD-1460]. Control-room staff could not carry out the isolation from where they could safely stand (L1) [FND-F887999D]. [FND-044D1DEC; FND-02310148; EVD-3932; FND-DBE08E73; FND-BDD73BA2; EVD-1460; FND-F887999D] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-09)*

### Detection that informs rather than acts

The fire and gas detection system was provided as an annunciator display with no automatic shutdown or suppression, and was never upgraded (L4) [FND-01C3DE98; FND-4458E0C0; EVD-3452]. No policy required it to be interlocked (L3) [FND-4D7CF773]. The detection lagged the people who saw the cloud by 5 minutes and left a fully manual response (L2) [FND-B5C0CE8F; EVD-4286]. The alarm at 02:20 started a manual search, not a protective action (L1) [FND-3D785319; EVD-8657]. [FND-01C3DE98; FND-4458E0C0; EVD-3452; FND-4D7CF773; FND-B5C0CE8F; EVD-4286; FND-3D785319; EVD-8657] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-10)*

## Breaks in the chain

Where the story needs a link the graph does not hold. Each is a gap, not a finding.

### Ignition

The ignition pathway is not among the top 40 links. The findings name a damaged flame-proof lamp, with three bolts missing and its glass case open, as the probable ignition source, under a breakdown-led electrical maintenance philosophy [FND-1280E788; FND-784A23E4; EVD-6916]. The export does not confirm the source. [FND-1280E788; FND-784A23E4; EVD-6916] *(evidence: direct)*

### Calibration change

A setting on the meter was changed and reversed during calibration in December 2019 without a record [FND-0B651843; EVD-2506]. The graph holds no link from this change to the seal failure, and the export does not say whether it bore on the joint. [FND-0B651843; EVD-2506] *(evidence: direct)*

### Pressure before the release

The pressure transmitters on the metering lines delivered no data, so no record shows how pressure behaved in the lines before the seal failed [FND-0D4C2C13; EVD-6470]. The chain from the untested joint to the moment of failure therefore has no measured link. [FND-0D4C2C13; EVD-6470] *(evidence: direct)*
