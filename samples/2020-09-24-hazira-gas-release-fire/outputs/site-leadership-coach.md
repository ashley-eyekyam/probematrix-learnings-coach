### Maintenance and spares programme

The maintenance and spares programme, one of the two largest hubs in the causal links, did not supply a specification for the 1986 meter's gasket or a reliability review of repeat failures. Supervision therefore could not act on the risk it saw, rules on the job were not enforced, and checklists were not followed at return to service. The pathway ends on a seal that was not to specification and was never tested to design pressure [FND-1A251687; FND-B440C1BF; FND-5919B328; EVD-8645; EVD-2894]. *(evidence: corroborated; +2 supporting; learning: LR-01)*

### Culture and risk practice

A culture that allowed unsafe work and applied risk management unevenly leads to gaps in the guidance supervisors gave and to routine deviations at the job. The findings read this as norms, not choices by one person: the substitute O-ring was 'regular practice', and the change procedure was not used after 2018. The pathway ends on changes to the plant that nobody reviewed [FND-8083ECAC; FND-B440C1BF; EVD-2986; EVD-8645]. *(evidence: direct; +5 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

### Command and control resources

Command and control resources and the timing of upgrades lead to equipment that was not set up for an emergency and to automation that was not provided. The isolation valves had no remote operation and the fire and gas system had no executive action. The pathway ends on an isolation that depended on people reaching the valve through the cloud [FND-044D1DEC; FND-6155A1C2; FND-9B406A83; EVD-3932]. *(evidence: corroborated; +6 supporting; learning: LR-09)*

## Culture signals

### What had become normal

Fitting a locally bought O-ring to a rectangular-slot flange was described as regular practice, repeated across three replacements in about 22 months, with no specification, quality check or recorded decision. Accepting a repaired pressure boundary after an operating-pressure soak had also become the modelled return-to-service practice [FND-B440C1BF; FND-A443D288; FND-71EE9734; EVD-8645; EVD-1494]. *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-01)*

### What was tolerated

Breakdown repair was the accepted approach for flameproof fittings in the Zone 1 area, and twelve external-audit recommendations ran on extended dates with no record of the approval the procedure required. Both were visible in site records [FND-1280E788; FND-856F80CE; EVD-6916; EVD-6852]. *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-11)*

### Where challenge was absent

The emergency structure ran as a serial chain: perimeter security, CCTV room, gas terminal control room, shift in-charge, overall shift in-charge, then plant leadership. No role below the plant manager held standing authority to close the isolation valve, so each step reported up rather than acted [FND-82761CC5; FND-A206DFD6; FND-4245FBF2; EVD-3932; EVD-6414]. *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-07)*

## Decisions for the leadership team

### Isolation authority

**Decision:** Give shift crews standing written authority to isolate and shut down on criteria written into the emergency plan [FND-82761CC5; FND-314D34C5; FND-AE629313; EVD-4052]
- Options: Shift in-charge isolates on defined criteria and reports afterwards; Control-room operator isolates on a confirmed release, shift in-charge confirms
- Trade-offs: Delegation removes the escalation delay but means accepting some isolations that later prove unnecessary, with lost production; the leadership team has to back crews who isolate in good faith.
- Owner: Operations function

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-07)*

**Indicator:** Time to isolation in drills [FND-81E3EEA0; FND-500E0B2D; EVD-6414]
- Measure: Time from a confirmed release to first isolation in each emergency drill
- Deterioration looks like: A drill in which isolation waits for a call above the shift in-charge
- Owner: Operations function

*(learning: LR-07)*

### Remote isolation

**Decision:** Needs corporate decision: fund remote operation of the emergency isolation valves for inventories that can form a large vapour cloud [FND-044D1DEC; FND-4C645CB5; FND-31405D96; EVD-3932]
- Options: Remote operation from the control room; Automatic closure on confirmed gas detection where justified
- Trade-offs: Remote operation is likely to need capital and a shutdown window; without it, isolation still depends on reaching the valve through the release.
- Owner: Engineering function

*(+5 supporting; learning: LR-09)*

**Indicator:** Remotely operable isolation [FND-9B406A83; FND-044D1DEC; EVD-3932]
- Measure: Share of emergency isolation valves on large inventories that can be closed from the control room
- Deterioration looks like: No change over two quarters once funding is decided
- Owner: Engineering function

*(learning: LR-09)*

### Fire and gas executive action

**Decision:** Needs corporate decision: approve fire and gas voting logic with automatic executive action for major-release areas [FND-6155A1C2; FND-4D7CF773; EVD-8657]

- Trade-offs: Automatic action brings spurious-trip risk and engineering cost; display-only detection left the response to a manual chain.
- Owner: Engineering function

### Pressure-part specifications

**Decision:** Give one function ownership of pressure-part specifications and of approving any substitute part [FND-B440C1BF; FND-DE30BD8B; EVD-8645]

### Return to service

**Decision:** Treat return to service after pressure-boundary work as a hold point that operations accepts in writing against a test record [FND-5919B328; EVD-2894; EVD-0185]
- Options: Hold point with a design-pressure test record; Hold point with an engineering-approved alternative test
- Trade-offs: A hold point adds time before restart; without it the meter went back online after an operating-pressure soak only.
- Owner: Operations function

*(learning: LR-02)*

**Indicator:** Restarts with a test record [FND-5919B328; EVD-2894]

- Deterioration looks like: Any restart without one
- Owner: Operations function
