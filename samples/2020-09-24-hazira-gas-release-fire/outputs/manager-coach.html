Bottom line

  • Several controls existed only on paper: a change procedure not used since 2018, an emergency plan without isolation criteria, and audits that recorded compliance without data.
  • No function owned the specification for a legacy pressure seal, so a substitute part became regular practice.
  • No procedure required a design-pressure test before pressure-boundary work went back into service.
  • Records held the warnings (repeat seal changes, wall loss, an open notification), but no system turned them into a decision.
  • Emergency isolation depended on reaching hand-operated valves inside the release area.

Operations & Control Room

11

What your system allowed

Emergency isolation depended on reaching valves that could only be closed by hand inside the release area. Owner: Operations function

The gas terminal isolation valves had no remote operation from the control room. When the vapour cloud denied access, the first isolation came only after a senior role reached the gate and the valve could be closed by hand. The operating system assumed access would be possible.

Evidence: corroborated
+8 supporting

Once the cloud blocked access, the shift response stayed on locating the leak rather than switching to upstream isolation. Owner: Operations function

Nothing in the plan or at the console told the shift when to stop searching and isolate from a distance. Each report went up the chain while people tried to approach the source on foot in very poor visibility.

Evidence: corroborated
+5 supporting

Control-room staff held emergency duties without a defined qualification. Owner: Operations function

New and transferred staff received a four-day general course and then on-the-job training; no qualification was defined in the HR or operations policy and there was no simulator practice. The rostering system had nothing to check against.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

Interfaces you share

With Mechanical Maintenance (the largest interface, 71 shared findings): agree that operations accepts a pressure-boundary job back into service only against a signed test record; the meter went back online after an operating-pressure soak [; ; ; ].

With Emergency Planning & Command: agree the isolation criteria, the valve list and the shift roles that may isolate without calling up the chain, and drill them together [; ; ; ].

Mechanical Maintenance

12

What your system allowed

A pressure-boundary seal was replaced with an unspecified local part because no one held the specification. Owner: Maintenance function

The 1986 meter's original gasket specification was not on file, so locally bought O-rings were fitted as regular practice, with no specification or quality check at purchase and no recorded decision to switch. The job had to be done, and the export shows nothing in the system that said stop.

Evidence: corroborated
+8 supporting

Pressure-boundary work went back into service without a test to design pressure, because no procedure required one. Owner: Maintenance function

After the gasket change on 18 September 2020 the system was pressurised to operating pressure only, and the meter was taken online the next day. No procedure set a test type, pressure or witness for these high-pressure meters.

Evidence: direct
+6 supporting

Repeat failures of the same seal were treated as routine repairs, not as a reliability warning. Owner: Maintenance function

The gasket was changed in November 2018, February 2019 and September 2020. Each job closed as a repair; no repeat-failure review existed to open a root-cause investigation.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

A high-consequence repair depended on one person's judgement, with no independent check of part, method or test. Owner: Maintenance function

The gasket job was planned with no inspection, test-witness or close-out role and without a job safety analysis, so the substitute part and the missing test reached service unchecked.

Evidence: corroborated
+2 supporting

Interfaces you share

With Work Management & Planning: agree that pressure-boundary jobs are planned as safety-critical, with test and witness steps written into the job plan [; ].

With Engineering, Design, Documents & MOC: agree who re-establishes a lost specification and who approves a substitute part, and that a substitute triggers a change review [; ; ; ].

Recommendations

Configure the work-order system so a pressure-boundary job cannot be closed, or the equipment released to operations, until a test result is entered.

Engineering, Design, Documents & MOC

9

What your system allowed

The management-of-change procedure existed on paper but had not been used since 2018. Owner: Engineering function

The procedure was published in August 2016, yet no change went through it after 2018, including the switch from the original gasket to a local O-ring. The export shows no prompt in the work or stores systems that treats a like-for-unlike part as a change.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

The metering P&ID was a lost original replaced by an unapproved vendor drawing that did not match the field. Owner: Engineering function

Old drawings were lost and a vendor drew new ones, which were never approved and did not match the valves and meters on site. Maintainers had no verified reference for the gasket job.

Evidence: direct
+4 supporting

With Operations & Control Room: agree which safety-critical drawings operations relies on in an emergency, so those are field-verified first [; ].

Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection

9

What your system allowed

Known pipe-wall loss was measured but not acted on: no corrective plan for three years, and painting used as the fix. Owner: Asset integrity function

The 2017 thickness survey found pipes needing corrective action, and three years later there was no plan. A July 2019 spot check found 1.03 mm of wall loss with heavy pitting on the 36-inch line, and painting was the corrective action. Measuring was in place; deciding was not.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

The four-year thickness inspection cycle slipped to six years, and baseline data and pipe schedules were missing. Owner: Asset integrity function

The last two surveys were in 2011 and 2017, and for many gas terminal pipes the baseline thickness and original drawings were not available. The schedule had no escalation when it slipped.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

Interfaces you share

With Mechanical Maintenance: agree that every wall-loss finding gets a repair, re-rate or replace decision before any painting closes it [; ; ].

With Operations & Control Room: agree how overdue integrity actions on lines in service are reported to the shift and to site leadership [; ].

Recommendations

Carry out fitness-for-service assessments on the 2017 and 2019 wall-loss locations, then repair, re-rate or replace as required.

Control: engineeringBarrier: prevention: pipeline integrityOwner: Asset integrity function

HSE, Process Safety & Audit

10

What your system allowed

Safety audits used generic checklists and recorded compliance without site data, so they could not find plant-specific defects. Owner: HSE and process safety function

The self-safety audit checklist was not related to the specific plant and recorded compliance where the data was later found incomplete; internal audits kept no filled checklists. The audit method could not have found the seal, test or isolation gaps.

Evidence: direct
+8 supporting

Maintenance notifications were treated as work permits, permits went missing untracked, and job safety plans were absent. Owner: HSE and process safety function

Some work permits were missing with no tracking, and job safety plans were not attached in the work system, so hazardous maintenance went ahead without a documented hazard analysis.

Evidence: direct
+5 supporting

Fire drill records held no actions or observations, so drills could not show whether readiness was improving. Owner: HSE and process safety function

Drill reports in the work system held dates only, and a separate record was not in line with the approved guidelines.

Evidence: direct
+5 supporting

Interfaces you share

With Mechanical Maintenance: agree that work opening a hydrocarbon pressure boundary needs its own permit and job safety analysis, separate from the maintenance notification [; ].

With Emergency Planning & Command: agree one drill record with timings for detection, notification and isolation, so drills test the isolation decision [; ].

Recommendations

Make the electronic permit mandatory for hydrocarbon work with an attached job safety analysis, and reconcile issued against closed permits daily.

Control: engineeringBarrier: prevention: permit to workOwner: HSE and process safety function

Leading indicatorUnreconciled permits per week, trending to zero; share of permits with a job safety analysis attached.

Work management: planners and schedulers

4

The malfunction notification was never closed or reviewed, so the last administrative check before return to service did not happen. Owner: Work management function

A notification for the meter was raised on 17 September 2020 and was not closed after the gasket job; no one was assigned to review the closure.

Evidence: direct

Pressure-boundary work was planned like any other repair, without inspection, witness or job safety steps. Owner: Work management function

The gasket job plan had no independent check, test witness or job safety analysis, so the planning system did not put a verification step between the fitter and the restart.
