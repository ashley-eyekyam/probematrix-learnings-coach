Bottom line

  • Add the threat of a non-specified seal on the 1986 metering skid: the original gasket was replaced by locally bought O-rings as regular practice, with no record of who decided it.
  • Revisit the rating of the emergency isolation controls: the plan gave no isolation criteria or authority, and the isolation valves could be closed only by hand inside the vapour cloud.
  • Revisit the rating of management of change: the procedure was published in 2016 but not used after 2018.
  • Twelve external-audit actions were carried past their dates without the approval the procedure requires: record them as risks accepted without authority.
  • Add key risk indicators for repeat failures, overdue integrity inspections and changes made without an MOC record, so the register shows these conditions before the next event.

Key risk and control indicators

6

Repeat failures on safety-critical equipment

Measure

Count of second and later failures of the same item within 24 months, from the maintenance notification history.

Deterioration looks like

Any item with a third failure and no reliability review on record.

Integrity inspections overdue

Measure

Share of thickness and integrity inspections past their scheduled cycle, from the inspection plan.

Deterioration looks like

Any inspection more than one cycle overdue.

Inspection findings without a corrective plan

Measure

Number and age of inspection findings that call for corrective action and have no approved plan.

Deterioration looks like

A finding open across two survey cycles, or closed by a coating where wall loss was found.

Changes with an MOC record

Measure

Share of material, equipment and setting changes in the work-order system that carry an MOC record.

Deterioration looks like

No MOC record raised in a quarter in which changes were made.

Fire-water pump availability

Measure

Share of time each fire-water pump is available, from test runs and maintenance records.

Deterioration looks like

A pump out of service, or a trip with no cause found, beyond the period the site allows.

Audit actions past their date

Measure

Number of audit actions past their target date, and how many of the extensions carry the required approval.

Deterioration looks like

Any extension with no approval on record.

Prevention

design and specification (substitution of parts)

Control: administrativeType: proceduralState: absent

Failure mode

No gasket specification was held for the 1986 meter, so locally bought O-rings with no specification or quality check were the regular substitute (L4).

Fix

Hold the specification for each pressure-boundary seal and block the issue of parts without one.

Language check JC6 accepted: the administrative change is the system fix at the level of the cause: a held specification and a stores block on unspecified parts

change management (MOC)

Control: administrativeType: proceduralState: defeated

Failure mode

The MOC procedure existed but was not used after 2018, so the seal substitution never reached an engineering review (L4).

Fix

Raise an MOC from the work-order and stores systems whenever a part differs from its specification.

Language check JC6 accepted: the administrative change is the system fix at the level of the cause: the MOC trigger is built into the work-order and stores systems

maintenance and testing (testing after maintenance)

Control: administrativeType: proceduralState: absent

Failure mode

No test to design pressure was required or done after the September 2020 gasket change, and no procedure covered the return to service (L3).

Fix

Make a recorded pressure test a hold point before a pressure-boundary item returns to service.

Language check JC6 accepted: the administrative change is the system fix at the level of the cause: a recorded test as a hold point before return to service

inspection and integrity (acting on inspection results)

Control: administrativeType: proceduralState: degraded

Failure mode

Wall loss was measured but not acted on for three years, and painting was used for pitted wall loss (L4, L3).

Fix

Require a fitness-for-service decision on every wall-loss finding, with the risk owner told of any finding left open.

Language check JC6 accepted: the administrative change is the system fix at the level of the cause: a fitness-for-service decision on every wall-loss finding
