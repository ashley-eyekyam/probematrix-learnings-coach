## Bottom line

- The release came from a pressure-boundary seal fitted without a specification and put back into service without a test to design pressure: check whether your site carries the same two conditions. [FND-82673283; FND-9174A5B0; EVD-8645; EVD-2894] *(learning: LR-01)*
- Isolation took about 46 minutes, because the plan held no isolation criteria and the valves could be closed only by hand inside the vapour cloud: check your emergency plan and isolation valves next. [FND-82761CC5; FND-05841EDA; EVD-4052; EVD-3932] *(learning: LR-07)*

## About this self-assessment

A self-assessment for each site. Results show coverage and conditions, never a ranking of sites.

## How to use it

The HSE lead at each site works through every self-check with the people who do the work, against the site's own records and plant. Look for the condition, not for a repeat of the event: the same condition can sit behind a different leak, a different valve or a different fire.

Record one answer for each self-check on the site's return: present, partly, absent or n/a. Add the site's response and the role of the person who assessed it. The organisation sets the date for returns.

## Self-checks

**Condition to look for:** A pressure-boundary seal or gasket is replaced with a part that has no written specification, or with a substitute no one has approved.
**Learning:** LR-01
**Evidence to check:** For each pressure-boundary seal or gasket fitted in the last two years, a specification or an approved substitute is on file and matches the part that was bought.
**Where to look:** Spares and purchase records, the stores catalogue and the maintenance history of older equipment [FND-82673283; FND-23139BF9; FND-A0017E28; FND-6B3A6492; EVD-8645; EVD-1494]

**Condition to look for:** Pressure-boundary work goes back into service without a test to design pressure, because no procedure requires one.
**Learning:** LR-02
**Evidence to check:** Recent seal, gasket or flange jobs on pressure equipment each have a test record that names the test type and pressure, and a return-to-service procedure requires it.
**Where to look:** Work orders, test certificates and the procedure index [FND-9174A5B0; FND-137BC719; FND-491EDE74; FND-50C7319E; EVD-2894; EVD-0185]

**Condition to look for:** A repair on a high-consequence pressure boundary rests on one person's judgement, with no independent check of the part, the method or the test.
**Learning:** LR-03
**Evidence to check:** Job plans for these repairs name a check of the part, a witnessed test and a close-out review, and the records show each was done.
**Where to look:** Job plans, permits and inspection or witness-point records [FND-28F8790B; FND-F8C125D5; FND-615DB819; FND-63B6D260; EVD-2894; EVD-5105]

**Condition to look for:** Repeat failures of the same component are repaired as routine each time, with no root-cause review or engineering look at why they recur.
**Learning:** LR-05
**Evidence to check:** The maintenance system can list the components repaired three or more times in two years, and each one has a root-cause review or an engineering decision on record.
**Where to look:** The failure history in the maintenance management system and the reliability or engineering review log [FND-69067A1C; FND-110F964C; FND-E0AE86A6; FND-1A251687; EVD-1494; EVD-0591]

**Condition to look for:** The management-of-change procedure exists on paper, but a different part, material or pipe route goes in without it.
**Learning:** LR-06
**Evidence to check:** The MOC register shows recent entries, and a sample of recent part or material substitutions each has an MOC record.
**Where to look:** The MOC register, recent purchase substitutions and modification work orders [FND-8083ECAC; FND-F7FE8463; FND-5A5F6AB3; FND-3FEE59DF; EVD-2986]

**Condition to look for:** The emergency plan names a major release but gives no criteria for when to isolate and no standing authority for the shift to order it.
**Learning:** LR-07
**Evidence to check:** For each major release scenario, the plan states the isolation trigger, the valves to close and which shift role may order isolation without waiting for a senior manager.
**Where to look:** The on-site emergency plan, its checklists and the control-room standing orders [FND-81E3EEA0; FND-BB4CC662; FND-82761CC5; FND-730D0DCA; EVD-4052; EVD-3932]

**Condition to look for:** When a release blocks access, the response plan still sends people to find the leak instead of switching to isolation from a safe place.
**Learning:** LR-08
**Evidence to check:** Emergency drills and the control-room decision aids include a release that blocks access, and drill records show the switch to remote or upstream isolation.
**Where to look:** Drill reports, emergency scenario cards and the control-room console aids [FND-305216AF; FND-4245FBF2; FND-C92C29DB; EVD-6414; EVD-7340; EVD-9612]

**Condition to look for:** Emergency isolation valves can be closed only by hand at the valve, inside the area a release would make unreachable.
**Learning:** LR-09
**Evidence to check:** The list of emergency isolation valves shows for each whether it can be closed remotely, and the risk that a release denies access to it has been assessed.
**Where to look:** The isolation valve list, the P&IDs and the site's risk assessment for releases [FND-4C645CB5; FND-DBE08E73; FND-05841EDA; FND-044D1DEC; EVD-3932; EVD-9612]

**Condition to look for:** Gas detectors only raise an alarm: they start no shutdown, isolation or protection, and their readings are not kept.
**Learning:** LR-10
**Evidence to check:** The fire and gas cause-and-effect chart shows which detectors trip a shutdown or start protection, and the control system stores detector history.
**Where to look:** The fire and gas cause-and-effect chart, the control system configuration and the alarm history [FND-A245072B; FND-D45021AD; FND-2D90823E; FND-6155A1C2; EVD-8657; EVD-3452]

**Condition to look for:** Damaged flameproof electrical equipment stays in service in a hazardous area, because electrical maintenance waits for breakdowns.
**Learning:** LR-11
**Evidence to check:** Each item of hazardous-area electrical equipment has its own inspection record within the inspection interval, and the defects found are closed out.
**Where to look:** The hazardous-area equipment register, the electrical inspection records and a walk of the hazardous areas [FND-784A23E4; FND-554BFB4A; FND-ABF11182; FND-5E08D0F8; EVD-6916; EVD-0496; EVD-9999]

**Condition to look for:** Control-room staff hold emergency duties with no defined qualification and no simulator or practised emergency training.
**Learning:** LR-12
**Evidence to check:** A control-room qualification is defined, and each person on the emergency roster has met it, with simulator or drill practice on record.
**Where to look:** Training records, the competence framework and the control-room roster [FND-337CB67F; FND-7885BCF5; FND-954553FD; FND-E4596837; EVD-5634; EVD-1460; EVD-9220]

## After the assessment

The organisation brings the returns together into one view: for each learning, how many sites assessed it, and for each condition, how many sites found it present or partly. The view never ranks or scores a site. A site that finds a condition present has done what the assessment asks, and its response goes into its own action process.
