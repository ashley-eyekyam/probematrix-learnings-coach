## Bottom line

- A pressure-boundary seal was replaced with an unspecified local part as regular practice, because no role held or owned the specification. [FND-23139BF9; FND-8CE00707; EVD-8645] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-01)*
- Written controls existed on paper but were not working: the management-of-change procedure had not been used since 2018, and the emergency plan gave no isolation criteria or delegated authority. [FND-F7FE8463; FND-5C7B163D; EVD-2986; EVD-4052] *(evidence: direct)*
- Repeat seal failures and measured pipe-wall loss were known before the event, but neither reached a decision that changed the plant. [FND-1A251687; FND-47B1702F; EVD-1494; EVD-5527] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-05)*
- The protective design depended on people reaching the release: gas detection only raised an alarm and the emergency isolation valves could only be closed by hand at the valve. [FND-4458E0C0; FND-044D1DEC; EVD-8657; EVD-3932] *(evidence: corroborated; learning: LR-09)*
- Assurance reported compliance that the evidence did not support: audits used generic checklists, and overdue external-audit recommendations were extended without the approval the procedure required. [FND-303495E9; FND-1D713648; EVD-9692; EVD-6852] *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-20)*

## What happened and why it matters to the board

In the early hours of 24 September 2020, condensate vapour escaped from a flange seal on a 1986 metering skid at the gas terminal. The vapour cloud was seen by perimeter security before the detection system alarmed, and the first emergency isolation valve was closed about 46 minutes after detection. The cloud ignited at 03:08:15, and a damaged flameproof light fitting was the probable ignition source [FND-044D1DEC; FND-CCE6F868; FND-01C3DE98; FND-D1DF4895; EVD-1284; EVD-6414; EVD-6916].

For the board, the event is a test of oversight more than an equipment failure. The conditions behind it (lost part specifications, unused change control, an emergency plan without isolation criteria, detection that only displayed alarms) were in place for months or years, and the findings record that none had been assessed as a risk needing a decision [FND-B440C1BF; FND-2D90823E; FND-05841EDA].

## What the investigation says about the management system

### Assurance that did not detect

The self-safety audit checklist was not specific to the plant and recorded compliance where the data was later found incomplete. The 2018 and 2019 internal audits kept no filled checklist, so their coverage cannot be checked. Audit results looked reassuring while plant-specific defects stayed in place [FND-303495E9; FND-48967DB2; FND-C0466EB3; EVD-9692; EVD-7681]. *(evidence: direct; learning: LR-20)*

### Signals that did not escalate

The same meter seal was replaced three times in about 22 months, and a 2017 thickness survey found pipes needing corrective action with no plan three years later. Both were recorded, but each was handled as routine work inside a function instead of as a risk for a management decision [FND-1A251687; FND-82FC718D; FND-47B1702F; EVD-1494; EVD-5527]. *(evidence: direct; +10 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

### Risk accepted without documented authority

Three kinds of risk were accepted with no recorded decision: the switch from the original gasket to a locally bought O-ring, changes made outside the management-of-change procedure since 2018, and twelve external-audit recommendations whose target dates were extended without the approval the procedure required [FND-BC4FD086; FND-F7FE8463; FND-1D713648; EVD-8645; EVD-2986; EVD-6852]. *(evidence: direct; +12 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

### Design and resources

The fire and gas system was set up to display alarms only, with no automatic shutdown or suppression, and the emergency isolation valves had no remote operation from the control room. Isolation therefore depended on people walking into the release area, and the emergency plan gave them no criteria or delegated authority to isolate [FND-119E6CF7; FND-322112EE; FND-82761CC5; EVD-3932; EVD-4052]. *(evidence: corroborated; +14 supporting; learning: LR-10)*

### Competence

Control-room staff took on emergency duties after a four-day general course, with no qualification defined in the HR or operations policy and no simulator practice [FND-337CB67F; EVD-5634]. *(evidence: direct; +4 supporting; learning: LR-12)*

## Questions for directors to ask management

**Q: Which pressure-boundary parts across our sites have no specification on file, and who owns re-establishing them?** [FND-23139BF9; FND-B56701CA; EVD-8645]
- Why it matters: The gasket specification was lost and no role owned it, so a substitute part became normal practice.
- Good evidence looks like: A register of critical pressure-boundary spares by site, showing the specification and certificate on file and the function that owns each.
- Put to: Executive management

*(+21 supporting; learning: LR-01)*

**Q: How many changes has each site put through the management-of-change procedure in the last year?** [FND-F7FE8463; FND-8083ECAC; EVD-2986]
- Why it matters: The procedure existed from 2016 but no change had gone through it since 2018.
- Good evidence looks like: A count of MOC records by site and period, with a sample traced from a work order to its MOC record.
- Put to: Executive management

*(+1 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

**Q: Were earlier warnings acted on: the repeat seal replacements, the 2017 thickness findings and the 2018 external-audit recommendations?** [FND-1A251687; FND-47B1702F; FND-1D713648; EVD-1494; EVD-5527; EVD-6852]
- Why it matters: Each was on record before the event, and none led to a decision that changed the plant.
- Good evidence looks like: For each warning: when it was raised, who decided what, and the evidence that the action closed.
- Put to: Executive management

*(+11 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

**Q: Who approved each extension of an overdue audit recommendation, and is that approval on file?** [FND-1D713648; FND-B2AC0F8C; EVD-6852]
- Why it matters: Twelve of 308 recommendations from the February 2018 external audit were extended without the document showing the approval the procedure required.
- Good evidence looks like: A list of extended recommendations with the approving authority and the record of each approval.
- Put to: Executive management

*(learning: LR-21)*

**Q: Do our safety audits test plant-specific evidence, or do they record compliance against generic checklists?** [FND-303495E9; FND-C0466EB3; EVD-9692; EVD-7681]
- Why it matters: Audits here recorded compliance without site data and could not find plant-specific defects.
- Good evidence looks like: Audit checklists written for each plant, filled checklists retained, and a sample of 'compliant' findings re-tested by an independent reviewer.
- Put to: HSE and process safety function

*(learning: LR-20)*

**Q: At which sites does emergency isolation depend on a person reaching a valve inside the hazard area?** [FND-322112EE; FND-02310148; EVD-3932]
- Why it matters: The isolation valves here were local-manual only, and the vapour cloud kept people from reaching them.
- Good evidence looks like: A site-by-site list of emergency isolation valves with their mode of operation, and the decisions taken on those that need local access.
- Put to: Engineering function

*(+5 supporting; learning: LR-09)*

**Q: Which of our gas detection systems only raise an alarm, and what is the agreed response time for each?** [FND-4458E0C0; FND-01C3DE98; EVD-8657]
- Why it matters: Detection here only displayed an alarm, and it alarmed after people had already seen the cloud.
- Good evidence looks like: A list of fire and gas systems by site, with their executive actions and the result of the last functional test.
- Put to: Engineering function

*(+8 supporting; learning: LR-10)*

**Q: Does every emergency plan state when to isolate and who may decide it on a night shift?** [FND-82761CC5; FND-DF1144C3; EVD-4052; EVD-3932]
- Why it matters: The plan named the major-leak scenario but gave no isolation criteria or delegated authority.
- Good evidence looks like: Emergency plans with written isolation criteria and named delegated roles, and drill records showing those roles exercised.
- Put to: Site leadership

*(+12 supporting; learning: LR-07)*

**Q: How do we know control-room staff are qualified for emergency duties before they are rostered?** [FND-337CB67F; EVD-5634]
- Why it matters: Staff here took on emergency duties after a four-day general course, with no defined qualification.
- Good evidence looks like: A defined qualification for control-room emergency duties and the share of each shift that holds it.
- Put to: Site leadership

*(learning: LR-12)*

## Indicators for the board dashboard

**Indicator:** Critical spares with a specification [FND-23139BF9; EVD-8645]
- Measure: Share of critical pressure-boundary spares with a specification and certificate on file, by site
- Deterioration looks like: Any fall from the previous report, or any critical spare issued without a specification

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Indicator:** Change control in use [FND-F7FE8463; EVD-2986]
- Measure: Number of MOC records opened and closed per site per quarter, with a sampled check of field changes against them
- Deterioration looks like: A site reporting no MOC records in a quarter, or a field change found with no record

*(learning: LR-06)*

**Indicator:** Overdue safety actions [FND-1D713648; FND-47B1702F; EVD-6852; EVD-5527]
- Measure: Overdue audit recommendations and integrity actions, and the number extended without recorded approval
- Deterioration looks like: Any extension without recorded approval, or a rising count of overdue actions

*(learning: LR-21)*

**Indicator:** Repeat failures escalated [FND-82FC718D; EVD-1494]
- Measure: Repeat failures on the same safety-critical item that triggered an engineering review, as a share of all repeat failures
- Deterioration looks like: Any repeat failure closed as routine repair without a review

*(learning: LR-05)*

**Indicator:** Time to emergency isolation in drills [FND-82761CC5; FND-D1DF4895; EVD-6414]
- Measure: Time from a confirmed release to first isolation in drills, by site
- Deterioration looks like: A drill in which isolation waits for authority from above the shift

*(learning: LR-07)*
