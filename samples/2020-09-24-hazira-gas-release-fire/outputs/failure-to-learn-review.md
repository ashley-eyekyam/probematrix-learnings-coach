## How practice drifted

Fitting a locally bought O-ring in place of the original gasket became the regular practice on meter 1. Each seal job solved the day's leak and was closed as routine, so the repeat never looked like a change that needed review. [FND-B86F67F0; FND-8CE00707; FND-1A251687; EVD-8645; EVD-1494] *(+7 supporting)*

What the documents said and what the site did moved apart in small steps: a change procedure that stopped being used, drill results kept in an informal parallel record, and audits that recorded compliance without the data behind it. No single decision made the gap. [FND-922A04DB; FND-7F27ED51; FND-9E1AB5BA; EVD-2986; EVD-2130; EVD-9692]

## Learnings for the organisation

**Learning:** A repeat failure on the same item needs a route that reports it as a warning, not as another job. [FND-82FC718D; FND-3A34BAD3; FND-E0AE86A6; FND-71D631C1; EVD-1494; EVD-0591]

The seal jobs on meter 1 were each recorded and closed as routine maintenance [FND-82FC718D; FND-3A34BAD3; EVD-1494]. The analysis states that the risk of the recurring failure was never assessed [FND-E0AE86A6]: no step in the maintenance records turned the repeats into a review.

- Owner: Mechanical Maintenance (F02, T3)

*(evidence: corroborated; +3 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

**Learning:** An audit that records no checklist cannot find what it did not look at, so its silence is not assurance. [FND-5BA62765; FND-9E1AB5BA; FND-C9C77FB7; FND-FE78954C; EVD-7681; EVD-9692]

The 2018 and 2019 internal audits gave observations without a filled checklist [FND-5BA62765; EVD-7681], and the self-audit checklist was generic to the location [FND-C9C77FB7; EVD-9692]. Without a record of what was checked, the audits could not turn the plant's defects into findings.

- Owner: HSE, Process Safety & Audit (F11, T3)

*(evidence: direct; learning: LR-20)*

**Learning:** A finding that needs corrective action needs a plan with an owner, or measurement becomes the response. [FND-0F19C9FC; FND-30D45EFE; FND-6E13192C; FND-E5C4209D; EVD-5527; EVD-2397]

The 2017 thickness report called for corrective action, and three years later no plan existed [FND-0F19C9FC; FND-30D45EFE; EVD-5527]. When wall loss was found on the 36-inch line, painting was accepted as the fix without examining whether it was enough [FND-6E13192C; EVD-2397].

- Owner: Asset Integrity, Corrosion & Inspection (F05, T3)

*(evidence: direct; +3 supporting; learning: LR-17)*

**Learning:** Extending an overdue safety action needs the approval the procedure sets, or the extension becomes the norm. [FND-2A77BAFB; FND-9078AFA4; FND-856F80CE; FND-5A742738; EVD-6852]

Most recommendations of the February 2018 external audit were complied with; the rest were extended to 30/04/2021 with no record of the required approval [FND-2A77BAFB; FND-9078AFA4; EVD-6852]. No record shows that the role with that authority accepted the delay.

- Owner: Plant & Asset Leadership (F13, T4)

*(evidence: direct; +4 supporting; learning: LR-21)*

**Learning:** A published procedure is a learning only while it is used; when it falls out of use, the change it guarded goes unreviewed. [FND-922A04DB; FND-F7FE8463; FND-3FEE59DF; FND-CDE8E62D; EVD-2986]

The management-of-change procedure was published in August 2016 and, as the record states, no change went through it after 2018 [FND-922A04DB; FND-F7FE8463; EVD-2986]. The substitute seal on meter 1 was therefore never reviewed as a change [FND-CDE8E62D].

- Owner: Engineering, Design, Documents & MOC (F07, T3)

*(evidence: direct; +2 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

**Learning:** The learning cycle closes only when someone checks that a job or an exercise did what it was for. [FND-646BAC1F; FND-30C43927; FND-62EF1667; FND-BC32F8F7; EVD-0591; EVD-2130]

The notification for meter 1 was not closed or reviewed after the gasket job, and no role was assigned to review it [FND-646BAC1F; FND-30C43927; EVD-0591]. Fire-drill records held no actions or observations [FND-BC32F8F7; EVD-2130], so neither could show whether anything had improved.

- Owner: Work Management & Planning (F08, T3)

*(evidence: direct; +8 supporting; learning: LR-04)*

## A note on hindsight

This review knows that meter 1 leaked on 24 September 2020; the people at the time did not. Every missed opportunity says what the record showed at that moment, events whose meaning was clear only afterwards are left out, and each learning asks why the system did not turn a warning into a change, not who missed it. [FND-71D631C1; EVD-1494]
