## Bottom line

- No specification, no start; no recorded test, no hand-back: the seal job ran without either, and the seal it fitted is the probable release source. [FND-904FCA63; FND-59A2F497; EVD-2894; EVD-8645] *(learning: LR-02)*
- A substitute part that has become regular practice is still a change: ask for the change review before you authorise it. [FND-CAF9833D; FND-A443D288; EVD-2986] *(learning: LR-06)*
- Close the loop: a notification left open and a repeat repair left unescalated took away the last checks before the release. [FND-FA807C69; FND-110F964C; EVD-0591; EVD-1494] *(learning: LR-04)*
- On the night, isolation waited for escalation; supervisors need written criteria and authority to isolate, which the site has still to settle. [FND-8DAFDADC; EVD-4052] *(learning: LR-07)*
- Put each check on the form where the work is released, so it becomes the easy way rather than a reminder. [FND-59A2F497; FND-63B6D260; EVD-5105] *(learning: LR-03)*

## Before work starts

### Plan

- [ ] The part to be fitted matches a written specification for that item. No specification on file means the job waits until one is found or an approved substitute is recorded. *(before a pressure-boundary job is released)* [FND-CEE4B00B; FND-A443D288; EVD-8645] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-01)*
- [ ] A part, material or size different from the original carries a management-of-change number. A substitute chosen as regular practice is still a change. *(before any substitute or not-in-kind part is fitted)* [FND-CAF9833D; FND-5A5F6AB3; EVD-2986] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-06)*
- [ ] A work permit and a job safety plan are in place for the task. A maintenance notification is not a permit. *(before work on hydrocarbon equipment starts)* [FND-7E1F2B3F; FND-63B6D260; EVD-5105] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-22)*
- [ ] A second person other than the fitter is named to check the part, the method and the test. *(when planning a job that opens a pressure boundary)* [FND-59A2F497; EVD-8645; EVD-2894] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-03)*

### Oversee

- [ ] Someone on the crew knows the item's specification and the test the joint needs, or the job waits for technical support. *(when choosing the crew)* [FND-54180594; FND-852B7EC7; EVD-1494] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-01)*

### Correct

- [ ] The item's history is read before the job. A third repair on the same item goes to a root-cause review, not to another identical fix. *(before repeating a repair)* [FND-110F964C; FND-4EDE038A; EVD-1494] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

### Oversee

- [ ] A checklist with as-found and as-left values and a change log is issued with the job. *(before calibration of a critical high-pressure meter)* [FND-89D53630; FND-1DA75FF5; EVD-2506] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-16)*

### Plan

- [ ] The drawing is a controlled, approved copy that matches the valves and meters in the field; a mismatch is reported before the plan is used. *(before an isolation is planned from a drawing)* [FND-73312E54; FND-82687B50; EVD-0933] *(learning: LR-19)*

## Known problems

A repeat is a signal, not a routine. The same meter had its gasket changed three times in about 22 months, each time with the same substitute part, and none of the repairs was escalated for root cause. When the same item comes back a third time, raise a root-cause request with the maintenance planner and tell the engineering function, and do not accept the same fix again without that review. [FND-110F964C; FND-4EDE038A; EVD-1494] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

An inspection result needs a decision, not a coat of paint. Pitting and wall loss found on a zero-corrosion-allowance line in July 2019 were painted, with no review of whether painting was enough. When an inspection reports wall loss or pitting, send it to the integrity engineers for a fitness-for-service decision before the coating work is accepted. [FND-4722A2B8; FND-20034564; EVD-2397] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-17)*

A defect on standby equipment matters most when it is needed. Two of four diesel fire-water pumps were under maintenance and one tripped after about two minutes, with no cause found or action recorded. Record a trip or failed start the same shift, raise it as a safety-critical defect, and escalate to the fire-protection owner when a pump stays unavailable. [FND-D8BC7096; FND-694B2DA0; EVD-2432] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-23)*

## In an emergency

The findings show isolation delayed while the response escalated. These are questions for the site to settle, not authority supervisors already hold.

**Q: On a confirmed major hydrocarbon release, who on shift may isolate without calling up the chain, and on what criteria?** [FND-8DAFDADC; FND-7505B1AF; EVD-4052; EVD-7340]
- Why it matters: The on-site plan named hydrocarbon leakage but gave no isolation criteria, and isolation authority was not allocated to the on-shift crew; the response escalated by phone while the cloud grew.
- Good evidence looks like: Written isolation criteria and delegated authority for each release scenario, known to every shift and tested in a night-shift drill.
- Put to: Plant leadership (owner of the emergency plan)

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-07)*

**Q: Which valves isolate each section of the unit, and from where can they be worked if the area cannot be entered?** [FND-DBE08E73; EVD-3932; EVD-9612]
- Why it matters: The emergency valves could only be closed by hand at the valve, and the release made the area unreachable.
- Good evidence looks like: An isolation matrix for each area at the control-room console, showing which valves are local-only and the plan when access is denied.
- Put to: Operations function

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-09)*

**Q: Should the shift treat any outside report of vapour or noise as a confirmed release until it is proved otherwise?** [FND-C75F81B9; FND-57281AC5; EVD-1460]
- Why it matters: The control room heard of the release as "some vapour cloud or steam leakage", after it had been relayed and re-described.
- Good evidence looks like: A standing instruction and a standard release message, used in joint security and operations drills.
- Put to: Operations function, with the security function

*(+5 supporting; learning: LR-13)*

**Q: Who on each shift is qualified and practised to lead an emergency isolation?** [FND-42043FE6; FND-4B285961; EVD-5634]
- Why it matters: Control-room staff held emergency duties after general training only, with no defined qualification or simulator practice.
- Good evidence looks like: A defined control-room qualification used as a rostering rule, with a record per shift.
- Put to: HR, training and competence function

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-12)*

## What to refuse

### Make the safe way the easy way

- [ ] Decline to accept the equipment until the test record and the witness's signature are attached. Expect management to back the delay. *(when pressure-boundary work is handed back without a test record)* [FND-C4E06264; FND-904FCA63; EVD-2894; EVD-0185] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-02)*

### Plan

- [ ] Decline to start until the permit and job safety plan are issued. *(when hazardous work is about to run on a notification only)* [FND-EFC192D7; FND-7E1F2B3F; EVD-5105] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-22)*

### Oversee

- [ ] Decline the roster and ask for a qualified person on the shift. Expect the roster to change, not the risk to be accepted. *(when a crew member without emergency-isolation practice would be alone on emergency duty)* [FND-337CB67F; FND-42043FE6; EVD-5634] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-12)*
- [ ] Decline to sign it until the checklist and every change are recorded. *(when a calibration record arrives without a checklist or with a setting change left out)* [FND-693FEDD2; FND-1DA75FF5; EVD-2506] *(+6 supporting; learning: LR-16)*

## Recommendations and how supervisors will know they are working

**Recommendation:** Configure the work-order system so a pressure-boundary job cannot be closed, or the equipment released to operations, until a test result and the witness's name are entered. [FND-904FCA63; FND-5919B328; EVD-2894]
- Control tier: engineering
- Barrier: Return-to-service test
- Leading indicator: % of pressure-boundary jobs on the shift log released with a recorded test result and witness

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-02)*

**Recommendation:** Link equipment status to notification status, so equipment cannot be set available while its corrective notification is open. [FND-FA807C69; FND-2B53492C; EVD-0591]
- Control tier: engineering
- Barrier: Notification close-out review
- Leading indicator: Equipment in service with an open corrective notification, counted at each shift handover

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-04)*

**Recommendation:** Add a safety-critical maintenance category to work planning that adds inspection, test-witness and close-out review steps by itself. [FND-59A2F497; EVD-8645]
- Control tier: engineering
- Barrier: Independent verification of safety-critical maintenance
- Leading indicator: % of safety-critical jobs on the shift record with every verification step signed by someone other than the performer

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-03)*

**Recommendation:** Set a repeat-failure trigger in the maintenance system that opens a root-cause task when the same item needs a set number of corrective jobs within a set period (the maintenance function sets the numbers). [FND-110F964C; EVD-1494]
- Control tier: engineering
- Barrier: Repeat-failure review
- Leading indicator: Repeat repairs on the same tag escalated for root cause, counted from the shift records

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-05)*

**Recommendation:** Make the electronic permit mandatory for hydrocarbon work, with the job safety plan attached, and reconcile issued and closed permits every day. [FND-7E1F2B3F; FND-A3D2773A; EVD-5105]
- Control tier: engineering
- Barrier: Permit to work
- Leading indicator: Permits issued but not reconciled at the end of each shift

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-22)*

**Recommendation:** Issue calibration checklists for high-pressure meters with as-found and as-left values and a change log, verified by a second person. [FND-89D53630; FND-D4BB5251; EVD-2506]
- Control tier: administrative
- Barrier: Calibration record
- Leading indicator: % of critical calibrations on the shift record with a complete checklist and second-person check
- Why not stronger: A calibration management system that records configuration changes by itself is the stronger control and is in the Learnings Register; the checklist is the step a supervisor can put in place on the next job while that is decided.

*(+6 supporting; learning: LR-16)*

*Language check JC6 accepted: The checklist is the supervision-level system fix for an L3 checking gap; the engineering option is named in why\_not\_stronger.*

## Coaching conversations

**Scenario:** A fitter tells you the high-pressure meter is ready to box up. The original seal is not in stores, so the crew used the O-ring they always use. Operations want the meter back on line in the morning, and nobody can tell you the test pressure. [FND-A443D288; FND-59A2F497; FND-C4E06264; EVD-8645; EVD-2894]

1. What do you check before you sign the job?
2. What do you say to operations about the morning?
3. Who do you call for the specification and the test pressure?
4. How do you make sure the next crew does not face the same choice?

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Scenario:** A notification comes in for a meter malfunction. Looking at the history, you see the same meter's gasket has been changed twice before in under two years, and the last notification for it was never closed. [FND-4EDE038A; FND-6D3446CD; EVD-1494; EVD-0591]

1. Is this a repair job or a reliability problem?
2. Who do you escalate it to, and what do you ask for?
3. What would you want closed before this job starts?

*(learning: LR-05)*

**Scenario:** At night the control room gets a call about "some vapour cloud or steam leakage" near the metering area. The gas alarm sounds a moment later. Your engineers go out and come back saying they cannot get near the source. [FND-C75F81B9; FND-8DAFDADC; FND-DBE08E73; EVD-1460; EVD-4052; EVD-3932]

1. What is your first decision, and who makes it?
2. Which valves would isolate the release, and can anyone reach them?
3. Whom do you call, and while you wait, what do you do?
4. What would you want the emergency plan to have told you?

*(learning: LR-07)*
