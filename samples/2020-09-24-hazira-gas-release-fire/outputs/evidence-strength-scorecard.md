## Evidence that would strengthen confidence

**Q: Do stores issue records or purchase orders show when locally bought O-rings were first fitted to meter 310FT01, and what authority approved the switch?** [FND-23139BF9; FND-B86F67F0; EVD-8645; EVD-1494]
- Why it matters: One document, EVD-8645, supports 67 of 436 findings (15%), the largest concentration. FND-23139BF9 (no accountable owner for the gasket specification) rests on it alone.
- Good evidence looks like: Stores issue records and purchase orders for the O-rings (log records), set against the meter's gasket history (EVD-1494)
- Put to: Stores and procurement officer

*(learning: LR-01)*

**Q: Can an account from the maintenance supervisor or the shift in-charge confirm how return to service after a gasket change was decided in practice?** [FND-137BC719; FND-611A8BFC; FND-71EE9734; EVD-2894]

## What this scorecard cannot tell you
