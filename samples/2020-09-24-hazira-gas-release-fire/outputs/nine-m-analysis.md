## M1 Manpower/Mindpower

### c-m1-1

**Learning:** A high-consequence seal job was staffed with no independent check role, so the part, the method and the missing test passed straight into service. [FND-615DB819; FND-A6312B6B; EVD-2894]

Subcategory M1.08 Communication and Coordination: nobody on the job was set up to check it and hand it over. The 18 Sep 2020 gasket job had no independent quality-check role, no hydrotest witness and no closing supervisor (OIS 4.2) [EVD-2894]. System driver: the maintenance and spares programme (OIR 1.6, level 4) feeds the gap in enforcing existing rules (SVV 4.1), and no return-to-service procedure existed to staff the job against. Barrier state: absent.

*(+1 supporting; learning: LR-03)*

**Barrier:** Independent check of pressure-boundary work before return to service [FND-615DB819; EVD-2894]
- Failure mode: absent: no quality-check role, hydrotest witness or closing supervisor on the job
- State on the day: absent
- Control tier: administrative

*Language check JC6 accepted: the barrier records the administrative control the export shows was in place; the change for this condition is at the elimination tier*

**Recommendation:** Corrective: remove the field reseal task from this class of joint, so that no crew has to judge the part, the method and the test alone. Pressure-boundary seals on the metering skid are restored by exchanging a shop-built assembly that has already passed its pressure test. [FND-615DB819; FND-A6312B6B]
- Control tier: elimination
- Leading indicator: Pressure-boundary seal jobs on the skid done by assembly exchange, as a share of all such jobs

## M2 Medium

### c-m2-1

**Learning:** The vapour cloud made the leak area unreachable, and isolating the release depended on reaching it. [FND-BDD73BA2; FND-9B406A83; FND-01ADF0AA; FND-7D28BE30; EVD-1460]

Subcategory M2.09 Environmental Stressors and Extreme Conditions. Dense vapour kept operating staff from approaching the metering area to find and isolate the source, a 41-minute delay before remote isolation (PTE 2.3) [EVD-1460]. System driver: a culture that allowed the unsafe set-up (OIC 2.1, level 4) feeds a workspace that did not fit the operation (PTE 2.8): the emergency valves could only be worked by hand at the valve. Barrier state: degraded.

*(+1 supporting; learning: LR-09)*

**Barrier:** Access to isolate the release at its source [FND-BDD73BA2; EVD-1460]
- Failure mode: degraded: the vapour cloud blocked the approach
- State on the day: degraded
- Control tier: administrative

*Language check JC6 accepted: the barrier records the administrative control the export shows was in place; the change for this condition is at the elimination tier*

**Recommendation:** Preventive: remove the need for anyone to approach a release to isolate it. Every emergency isolation point for the metering area is worked from outside the area a release can cover. [FND-BDD73BA2; FND-7D28BE30]
- Control tier: elimination
- Leading indicator: Emergency isolation points on the skid that can be worked without entering the release zone

## M3 Method

### c-m3-1

System driver: the maintenance and spares programme (OIR 1.6, level 4) feeds risks that were identified but not mitigated (SVF 3.4). Barrier state: absent.

*(+1 supporting; learning: LR-06)*

- Failure mode: absent: not applied to any change since 2018

## M4 Mission

### c-m4-1

**Learning:** The site's working doctrine made risky conditions normal: no isolation criteria in the emergency plan, breakdown repair for flameproof equipment, and unspecified seals as regular practice. [FND-5C7B163D; FND-1280E788; FND-8CE00707; EVD-4052; EVD-9999; EVD-8645]

Subcategory M4.08 Organisational Culture and Mission Alignment, kept in M4 rather than M1 because it is what the organisation's doctrine made normal, not how a team was led day to day. The emergency plan named the major-leak scenario with no isolation criteria or authority [EVD-4052]; breakdown repair was preferred over preventive inspection in Zone 1 [EVD-9999]; and locally bought O-rings were 'regular practice' [EVD-8645] (OIP 3.5, three findings). System driver: formal risk assessment (SVIO 2.4, level 3) feeds mission planning (PTF 1.13). Barrier state: absent.

*(+2 supporting; learning: LR-07)*

**Barrier:** Emergency plan isolation criteria and delegated authority [FND-5C7B163D; EVD-4052]
- Failure mode: absent: the plan named the scenario but not when to isolate or who decides
- State on the day: absent
- Control tier: administrative

*Language check JC6 accepted: the barrier records the administrative control the export shows was in place; the change for this condition is at the elimination tier*

**Recommendation:** Corrective: remove the ignition sources the doctrine left to run to breakdown. Move electrical equipment that does not need to be in the Zone 1 metering area out of it, so a major leak there finds nothing to ignite while isolation is decided. [FND-1280E788; FND-5C7B163D]
- Control tier: elimination
- Leading indicator: Electrical items inside the Zone 1 metering area that do not need to be there (count)

## M5 Measurement

## M7 Machine

### c-m7-1

**Learning:** Ageing equipment stayed in service without upgrade or retirement: alarm-only gas detection, a 1986 meter with repeat seal failures, and emergency valves worked only by hand. [FND-5897D1E4; FND-55F68376; FND-6DB250E6; FND-89D53630; EVD-8657; EVD-1494; EVD-3932]

Subcategory M7.08 Lifecycle Management. Gas detection only displayed an alarm, with no shutdown or suppression interlock, so a leak detected at 02:12:50 became a 46-minute manual response [EVD-8657]; the 1986 meter stayed in service after three seal failures in 22 months [EVD-1494]; and the emergency valves were never upgraded to remote operation [EVD-3932] (OIR 1.7, three findings). System driver: leadership and supervisory oversight (SVI 1.1, level 3) feeds checklists not followed (ASBE 1.2). Barrier state: absent.

*(+2 supporting; learning: LR-10)*

**Barrier:** Automatic shutdown on confirmed gas detection [FND-5897D1E4; EVD-8657]
- Failure mode: absent: detection was annunciator-only
- State on the day: absent
- Control tier: engineering

**Recommendation:** Corrective: retire the 1986 meter and its rectangular-slot joint, removing the seal that failed four times, and replace it with a design whose seal is specified and stocked. [FND-55F68376; FND-5897D1E4]
- Control tier: elimination
- Leading indicator: Metering-skid joints in service with two or more seal failures in 24 months (count)
