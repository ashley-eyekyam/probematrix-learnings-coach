# Audience catalogue

Who learns from an investigation, what they control, and which skill serves them. Use this to route requests and to write in the right register.

| Audience | Typical people | What they control | Learning they need | Skill |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Frontline | operators, technicians, fitters, crews, security staff | how the task is done today | practical warning signs, what to stop and ask | `frontline-learning-cards` |
| First-line supervisors | shift in-charges, maintenance supervisors, permit issuers, signatories | which work starts, which work is accepted | checks, hold points, what to refuse | `supervisor-coach` |
| Function managers | maintenance, operations, engineering, integrity, HSE managers | procedures, resources, priorities in a department | system changes, leading indicators | `manager-coach` |
| Site / asset leadership | plant manager, head of operations, site leadership team | site priorities, budgets, culture signals | decisions, trade-offs, what leaders model | `site-leadership-coach` |
| Top management | CEO, ExCo, corporate HSE, corporate functions | policy, enterprise standards, investment | enterprise-wide transfer, policy and investment decisions | `top-management-brief` |
| Board | board, board safety / risk committee, independent directors | oversight, assurance, risk appetite | questions to ask, assurance gaps | `board-brief` |
| Auditors and assurance | internal audit, certification bodies, regulators' inspectors, insurers | assurance programmes | test points, evidence to request, sampling | `auditor-assurance-pack` |
| Investigators and HSE professionals | incident investigators, HSE advisers, process safety engineers | investigation quality | evidence gaps, key questions, interviews, method lessons | `investigator-coach` |
| Training and L&D | training managers, instructors, competence assessors | curricula, assessment | training needs, modules, assessment items | `training-designer` |
| Emergency response and fire services | fire crews, emergency planners, incident commanders | response plans, drills, readiness | detection-to-isolation, command, readiness learnings | `emergency-response-learnings` |
| Engineering and integrity | design, MOC, document control, integrity, reliability | technical standards, change, inspection | design, MOC and integrity learnings | `engineering-integrity-learnings` |
| Contractors and supply chain | procurement, stores, vendors, OEMs, contractors | specifications, sourcing, contractor work | sourcing, specification and contractor learnings | `supply-chain-contractor-learnings` |
| Workforce representatives | safety committee members, union safety reps | consultation, raising concerns | discussion guide, questions to raise | `safety-committee-guide` |
| HR and workforce planning | HR business partners, workforce planners, rostering and fatigue owners, competence-framework owners | selection and staffing standards, role qualifications, crew composition, rosters and workload | system learnings on selection, qualification and manning, and the competence framework to change | `people-workforce-learnings` |

Registers by audience: frontline short and concrete; supervisors checklists; managers systems and indicators; leadership decisions and trade-offs; board questions and assurance; auditors test criteria.
