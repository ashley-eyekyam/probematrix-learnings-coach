# Jurisdictions

A reminder of the regimes that may apply to an incident, and of the statutory notice or report the organisation may still owe. A learning pack is never that report: it carries "Learning purposes: not a statutory document".

How to use this file:
- This is a product reference, not legal advice. It says what a regime requires as summarised in the coach's research; it does not say whether a regime applies to an incident or whether a duty was met. **The organisation decides**, with its own legal and compliance advisers.
- Each section starts with a `Regimes:` line: a comma-separated list of the regimes whose statutory notice or report the organisation may still owe. The engine reads it to print "Statutory report still required: …" when a run names the jurisdiction. Other regimes are listed in the section body.
- Items marked *(unverified)* could not be confirmed against primary text. Check them before relying on them.
- Write learnings in just-culture language: roles only, no legal conclusions (the language check flags them). Never write that a duty was breached.
- Requirement IDs for packs (`clauses`) are in `standards-crosswalk.json`.

## in

Regimes: Factories Act 1948 s.88, OSH Code 2020 s.10, OSH Code 2020 s.11, MSIHC Rules 1989 Rule 5
Last checked: 2026-10-17

**What the regimes require**
- **Occupational Safety, Health and Working Conditions Code 2020 (OSH Code).** Commenced on 21 November 2025 (S.O. 5321(E)); Central Rules notified 8 May 2026 (G.S.R. 345(E)). Section 10 covers notice of accidents, s.11 notice of dangerous occurrences, and s.22 safety committees. Contract labour duties under the Code *(unverified)*.
- **Factories Act 1948.** s.41G (safety committee in hazardous-process factories) and s.88 / s.88A remain relevant to legacy events and to state transition rules. s.41B and s.41H are also named in the coach's research *(content unverified)*.
- **Manufacture, Storage and Import of Hazardous Chemical (MSIHC) Rules 1989, Rule 5.** Notify the concerned authority within 48 hours of a major accident, follow up with reports, and report the steps taken to avoid recurrence.
- **PNGRB ERDMP Regulations 2010** (amended 2015 and 2020; consolidated 2025). Apply to gas installations of the Hazira type. A 2026 amendment on post-major-incident committees and board reporting is reported by one secondary source *(unverified)*.
- **OISD.** Parliamentary answers state that the operator investigates every incident, OISD or a ministry committee investigates major ones, and root causes and lessons are shared across oil companies.
- **DGMS.** Mines Act 1952 s.23 notice of accidents, for mining exports *(unverified)*.
- **Board reporting.** Companies Act 2013 s.134 (Board's report, including risk management); SEBI BRSR Principle 3 indicators cover safety incidents and high-consequence injuries.

**Statutory report the organisation may still owe**
- The notice of accident or dangerous occurrence under OSH Code s.10 / s.11, or under Factories Act s.88 / s.88A for legacy events.
- For a major accident at an MSIHC site: the Rule 5 notification within 48 hours, the follow-up reports, and the report of steps taken to avoid recurrence.
- For a mine: the Mines Act notice *(unverified)*.
- Whether an ERDMP plan change must go to the authority is for the organisation to check.

**Sources**
- OSH Code 2020: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/22041 ; commencement: https://www.taxmann.com/post/blog/govt-notifies-enforcement-of-occupational-safety-health-working-conditions-code ; ss.10–11: https://taxclue.in/blog/section-10-11-osh-code-2020-notice-of-accidents-and-dangerous-occurrences/
- Factories Act 1948: https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/18999/1/factories_act_1948.pdf ; s.41G: https://www.advocatekhoj.com/library/bareacts/factories/41g.php

## uk

Regimes: RIDDOR, COMAH 2015
Last checked: 2026-10-17

**What the regimes require**
- **Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974 (HSWA)** and the HSE workbook **HSG245** (updated 2022): gather, analyse, identify risk-control measures, action plan and implementation. The coach covers analysis and risk-control measures; the action plan is the organisation's.
- **RIDDOR.** A statutory report the organisation may owe to the regulator. The coach's research names it but does not summarise its thresholds: check them against the regulations.
- **COMAH 2015 (GB).** Carries the Seveso III Annex III safety management system duties (monitoring performance, audit and review). The post-accident information regulation number differs between GB and Irish instruments, so it is not cited here.
- **Safety Representatives and Safety Committees Regulations 1977 (SRSC).** Union safety representatives may have statutory rights to investigation documents (reg.4 / reg.7) *(unverified)*. A committee guide does not replace those rights.
- **Board reporting.** UK Corporate Governance Code 2024 Provision 29 (periods from 1 January 2026): a declaration on the effectiveness of material controls and a description of any that did not operate effectively.

**Statutory report the organisation may still owe**
- The RIDDOR report, where the event falls within it.
- For a COMAH establishment: the post-accident information to the competent authority.

**Sources**
- HSG245: https://www.hse.gov.uk/PUBNS/books/hsg245.htm
- COMAH 2015: https://www.hse.gov.uk/comah/comah15.htm ; L111: https://www.hse.gov.uk/PUBNS/books/l111.htm
- UK Corporate Governance Code: https://www.frc.org.uk/directors/corporate-governance/uk-corporate-governance-code

## us

Regimes: OSHA PSM 29 CFR 1910.119(m), EPA RMP 40 CFR 68.81
Last checked: 2026-10-17

**What the regimes require**
- **OSHA Process Safety Management, 29 CFR 1910.119.**
  - (m) Incident investigation: investigate within 48 hours; the team includes a contract employee where contractor work was involved (m)(3); the report contains at minimum the date of incident, the date the investigation began, a description, contributing factors and recommendations (m)(4); a system promptly addresses and resolves findings, with resolutions documented (m)(5); the report is reviewed with all affected personnel, including contract employees (m)(6); reports are retained five years (m)(7).
  - (o) Compliance audits: at least every three years, a documented response to each finding (o)(4), and the two most recent reports retained (o)(5).
  - Other PSM element letters, such as (h) contractors, are named in the coach's research without primary-text checks *(unverified)*.
- **EPA Risk Management Program, 40 CFR 68.81** (Program 3) mirrors PSM (m), with five-year retention. The 2024 Safer Communities rule added a root-cause-analysis requirement. EPA's February 2026 proposal would roll back much of that rule but reportedly keeps the root-cause element, with compliance by 10 May 2027 (law-firm summary; check the final rule).

**Statutory report the organisation may still owe**
- The PSM (m)(4) incident investigation report, and the documented resolution of each finding under (m)(5).
- The RMP 68.81 incident investigation report for a covered process.

**Sources**
- OSHA 1910.119: https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.119
- EPA 40 CFR 68.81: https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-40/chapter-I/subchapter-C/part-68/subpart-D/section-68.81
- EPA 2026 proposal (secondary): https://www.haynesboone.com/news/alerts/epa-proposes-rollback-of-chemical-accident-prevention-rules

## eu

Regimes: Seveso III Directive
Last checked: 2026-10-17

**What the regimes require**
- **Seveso III Directive, Annex III safety management system.** (vi) monitoring performance: reporting and investigating major accidents and near misses, especially failures of protective measures, and the follow-up based on lessons learnt. (vii) audit and review.
- Member states apply the Directive through national instruments (for example the Irish COMAH Regulations 2015); the post-accident information duty and its regulation number differ between instruments.
- **Data protection.** GDPR applies to personal data in the export and the outputs: see `data-handling.md`.

**Statutory report the organisation may still owe**
- After a major accident: the information the national competent authority requires under the instrument that applies.

**Sources**
- Seveso III Annex III monitoring performance (JRC Minerva): https://minerva.jrc.ec.europa.eu/en/shorturl/minerva/safety_performance_indicators_28mjv_austria29finaldoc
- Irish COMAH 2015 guide: https://hsa.ie/eng/your_industry/chemicals/legislation_enforcement/comah/guidance/comah_regulations_2015
