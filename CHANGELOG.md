### 2.0.0a8

Phase 6: new skills, wave 2, and the library foundation. The last build phase of v2.0.

- **Seven new skills** (39 in all), each with a manual page, evals, a synthetic fixture and a Hazira worked example:
  - `safety-alert` (audience `other-sites`): a one-page alert graded alert, notice or information, in `internal`, `sister-site` or `industry` mode, as Word, a one-slide deck, an email and an A3 poster. The grade is the highest `urgency` the register gives the chosen learnings; when there is none, the agent asks the user, suggesting a grade from priority and transferability.
  - `could-it-happen-here` (audience `sister-sites-hse`): a self-check for each transferable learning, a return template for each site and a consolidated view of coverage and conditions. It carries "A self-assessment for each site. Results show coverage and conditions, never a ranking of sites."
  - `safety-moment-deck` (audience `session-leaders`): a 3 to 6 slide deck for a short session, with a one-page poster and optional narration and captions.
  - `learning-team-facilitator` (interactive): work as imagined against work as done, "what made sense at the time" questions, a 60 to 90 minute session plan and a blank notes sheet. It carries "The team supplies the answers. These are questions, not findings about people."
  - `restorative-response-guide` (audience `leaders-and-peer-support`): restorative questions to ask the people involved, the organisation's obligations and second-victim support, as questions only ("needs are asked, never inferred").
  - `systemic-accimap`: the findings on Rasmussen's six AcciMap levels, with the empty levels as gaps and a banner asking a person to confirm every placement above the company level.
  - `learning-effectiveness-plan`: what to check for each learning and when, counted from adoption, at the Kirkpatrick levels, with Jacobsson's spread and depth, and a leading and a lagging indicator per learning. It carries "The organisation sets owners, dates and statuses. This plan sets out what to check and when, counted from adoption."
- **New commands.** Each is deterministic, exits 0 with empty lists and a note on a thin export or register, and prints ready pack items.
  - `alert-grade` gives the alert's grade and `grade_source` for the default learnings (priority 1, ordered by transferability, evidence strength and LR-ID, top 3) or the `--lr` ones. On the Hazira register: `alert` for LR-01, LR-02 and LR-03, from the register's urgency.
  - `templates cihh --pack <could-it-happen-here.json>` writes `<pack>.cihh-template.xlsx`, one sheet `Return` with Site (B1) blank, the pack's content hash in B2 and one row per `self_check` item, answer, site_response and assessed_by_role blank. 11 rows for the Hazira pack.
  - `consolidate` reads the sites' returns and writes `outputs/cihh-consolidated.json` (returns, coverage per learning, conditions found present or partly, and the learnings each site assessed: nothing ranks a site) and `outputs/cihh-events.jsonl` (one `site_assessment` event per answered row). A bad answer, a blank site, two returns for one site, a return from another pack's template or a file that is not a return each give one line naming the file and row, and nothing is written unless `--keep-going` is given.
  - `wai-wad` writes `workspace/wai_wad.json`: per code, findings with document and with work-as-done evidence, the gaps, and the counterfactual findings (`--table-item gaps|codes`, `--items prompts --top N`). On Hazira: 68 of 94 codes and 92 findings with both, 210 with any work-as-done evidence, 337 counterfactual.
  - `templates learning-team` writes `learning-team-notes.csv`, header only (`prompt_id,prompt,team_answer,source`), for the team to fill in.
  - `accimap` writes `workspace/accimap.json`: each finding on an AcciMap level by the rules in `references/accimap.json`, inferred placements marked, empty levels as `gaps` (`--graph-item`, `--table-item levels`, `--map`). On Hazira levels 1 and 2 are gaps.
  - `effect-plan` writes `workspace/effect_plan.json`: the checks for each register learning as offsets from adoption ("90 days after adoption", never a date), with Kirkpatrick levels and Jacobsson's spread, depth and duration (`--table-item schedule|levels|indicators`, `--items audit`). On Hazira: 97 checks, 11 × 5 for priority 1 and 14 × 3 for the rest.
  - `library publish` adds a run's learnings to an opt-in learnings library (`--library` or `PMLC_LIBRARY`), with `--register`, `--site`, `--names`, `--orgs` and `--dry-run`. See the library notes below and `docs/manual/Library.md`.
- **New pack content (all optional).** The top-level `mode` (`internal`, `sister-site`, `industry`), `grade` (`alert`, `notice`, `information`; a badge under the title in every format) and `grade_source` (`register urgency` or `chosen by the user`); the `self_check` kind (`condition`, `evidence_to_check`, optional `lr_id` and `look_in`). New profile rules: `grade-source` (a warning), `alert-priority-1`, `leading-lagging`, `needs-are-questions` and `inferred-banner`. A profile may set `pptx_slides` to put every section on one slide (`1`) or one slide per section (`"sections"`).
- **Sharing an alert.** A `sister-site` alert rendered without `--share external`, or an `industry` alert without `--share public`, is the internal copy with "Review copy: not for distribution outside the organisation until it is approved and shared with --share external." first in every format. `--share external` of an industry alert is refused ("an industry alert is shared with --share public"). The existing approval gate applies to every masked copy.
- **Extensions.** `top-management-brief` points to `could-it-happen-here` for the sister sites, and the follow-up pointers lead to the effectiveness plan.
- **The learnings library (storage and publish only).** `library publish` appends to JSON Lines files in a folder you choose; it never edits or deletes a line. Each incident gets a stable key and each learning an `LL-` ID that it keeps when reworded; references carry their incident (`INC-…/FND-…`). An unchanged run appends nothing, a changed learning appends a superseding snapshot, and a learning that leaves the register gets a `withdrawn` event. Every public line passes a de-identification gate first: zero hits for listed names, the site coded `S01` …, the text masked as an external shared copy, dates rounded to the month, and a last scan. The organisation's own files (`cihh-events.jsonl`, `verification.jsonl`, `adoption.csv`) are added as events. Synthetic data only in this repository: no Hazira data is published into a library.
- **Workflows.** `first-48-hours` and `sister-sites` gain `safety-alert`, and `sister-sites` also `could-it-happen-here`; `training` gains `safety-moment-deck`; `investigation-quality` gains `wai-wad` and `learning-team-facilitator`, and `leadership` gains `accimap` and `systemic-accimap`, both only when the user asks; `follow-up` plans `learning-effectiveness-plan`, then the verification log.
- **Migration notes.**
  - **New optional pack fields.** `mode`, `grade` and `grade_source` are optional top-level fields in `pack.schema.json`. An invalid `grade` or `grade_source` is a schema problem in any pack; a missing `grade_source` on a graded pack is a warning only.
  - **The `self_check` kind** is new and optional; `templates cihh` writes one return row per `self_check` item.
  - **The library layout.** A library folder holds `library.json`, `learnings.jsonl`, `events.jsonl`, `incidents.jsonl` and `private/` (`sites.json`, `contexts.jsonl`, never shared). It is new and opt-in: without `library publish` nothing changes, and run folders are not touched.
  - **The sample register has `urgency`.** The Hazira sample register now gives each learning an `urgency`, so the safety-alert golden reads its grade from the register. It still passes `check-register --strict` with 0 problems.
  - **`artefacts.json`** gains `wai-wad`, `accimap`, `effect-plan`, `cihh-template`, `cihh-consolidated` and `learning-team-notes`; the `library` entry is no longer `planned`. `status` reports the three new workspace files stale after a re-ingest.
  - **The v2.0 scope changed.** v2.0 ends after Phase 6 with 39 skills. The five multi-incident skills planned for Phase 7 (learning-verification, repeat-learnings, portfolio-trends, change-and-task-lessons, lfi-health-check), and reading the library (`library export|match|search`, the portfolio views, a `sqlite3` index, small-cell suppression and the refusal to mix taxonomies), move to v2.1.
  - **Unchanged.** No flag, subcommand, pack field, kind, artefact name or output file name was renamed or removed. `pack` with no `--format` writes what it wrote in alpha.7, and alpha.7 and earlier packs still pass a non-strict `check-pack`. The Phase 4 and Phase 5 Hazira numbers do not move.

### 2.0.0a7

Phase 5: new skills, wave 1.

- **Nine new skills** (32 in all), each with a manual page, evals, a synthetic fixture and a Hazira worked example:
  - `evidence-strength-scorecard`: how well each finding is evidenced, by level, where the evidence is concentrated, and the evidence requests that would strengthen it. It carries "This scorecard shows where more evidence would strengthen confidence. It does not grade the investigation team."
  - `open-questions-register`: what the investigation could not answer, what would settle each question, and `open-questions-follow-up.csv` to paste back into the export.
  - `latent-conditions-profile`: the HFACS heat map, the codes that recur and the ones that feed the most others, with the line that one incident's counts are not rates, trends or a benchmark.
  - `failure-to-learn-review`: why earlier warnings did not lead to change, with one row per phase of Drupsteen's learning cycle and each event labelled by what was knowable then.
  - `nine-m-analysis` (interactive): the 9M interview, one question at a time with 2 to 4 suggestions and "None / unknown (record as a gap)", then one learning and direction of change per relevant M, `outputs/nine-m-map.json` and a blank actions sheet.
  - `recommendation-strength-review`: the register's (and the 9M analysis's) recommendations banded stronger, intermediate or weaker after the RCA² action hierarchy, weak fixes for deep causes flagged, single and double loops, the spread over the nine Ms, SMART gaps and up to ten directions for stronger change.
  - `people-workforce-learnings` (audience `hr-workforce`): people-system learnings and competence-framework changes, about systems, not individuals' records.
  - `risk-register-update` (audience `enterprise-risk`): proposed risk-register and bow-tie changes, as proposals for the risk owner, written to `<pack>.risk-changes.csv`.
  - `compliance-crosswalk`: which clauses of ISO 45001, PSM, RMP, Seveso, CCPS RBPS, ICMM or the Indian acts and rules the learnings speak to, as an aid to assurance, not a compliance opinion.
- **New commands.** Each writes a deterministic workspace artefact (byte-identical on a re-run), exits 0 with empty lists and a note on a thin export, and is reported stale by `status` after a re-ingest.
  - `evidence-audit` writes `evidence_audit.json`: each finding's strength (`direct`, `corroborated` or `inferred`), the single-source and document-only findings by level, and the evidence concentration (`--table-item scorecard|concentration|levels`). On Hazira: 203 single-source and 195 document-only findings, and EVD-8645 supports 67.
  - `hfacs-profile` writes `hfacs_profile.json`: the level by subcategory heat map, recurring codes and causal feeds (`--table-item heatmap|feeds|recurrence`).
  - `open-questions` writes `open_questions.json`: empty follow-up columns, blank Incident Summary fields, and the findings whose justification says something is absent or is hedged, from documented word lists in `references/open-questions.json` (`--items` prints ready `question` items).
  - `nine-m-seed` scores every finding against the nine Ms and prints the M profile (`nine_m_seed.json`). `references/nine-m.json` holds the 9 chapter names and 97 subcategory names of the ProbeMatrix 9M framework only, with newly written definitions and probes; action catalogues stay in the ProbeMatrix CAPA Zone. A CAPA Planner workbook is refused.
  - `nine-m-suggest`, `nine-m-answer` and `nine-m-replay` run the 9M interview and record it in `nine_m_session.json`; `nine-m-replay --accept-recommended` scripts a demonstration session, and the pack then carries "Answers chosen for demonstration, not by the organisation."
  - `nine-m-actions-template` writes `<pack>.actions-template.xlsx` (or `.csv`) from a nine-m-analysis pack, with Owner, Due date, Status and Notes blank; it never overwrites a sheet that exists.
  - `rec-review` bands a register's recommendations, flags a learning at HFACS level 3 or 4 with no stronger or intermediate change as `weak fix for a deep cause`, and counts loops and SMART gaps (`--nine-m`, `--table-item strength|spread|smart`). It reads a register, never a pack.
  - `missed-opportunities` writes `missed_opportunities.json`: the findings citing an earlier recommendation (107 on Hazira), the Failure-to-Correct findings (40), the dated precursors before the incident that cite them, and drift wording (`--timeline-item`).
  - `crosswalk` maps the register to a framework's clauses (`--framework iso45001|psm|rmp|seveso|rbps|icmm|india`, `--table-item`). `standards-crosswalk.json` gains the 20 CCPS RBPS elements and ISO 45001 clauses 4.1 to 10.3; a requirement without a recorded primary source is marked "(unverified)".
- **New flags.** `timeline-item --event-day` (one event per clock time in the incident narrative, 26 on Hazira); `select --m M3` and `--m-secondary` (select through the 9M lens); `--industry process` on `classify` and `setup` (the process-industries reference pack: role words, barriers, indicators and a glossary, printed as "method reference, not evidence"); `pack-xlsx` with `--kind table` (a sheet per table item, with its row cites); `pack --format risk-csv` (an existing `<pack>.risk-changes.csv` is never overwritten, because the risk owner records decisions in it); `table-item nine-m`.
- **New pack content (all optional).** The `risk_change` kind; the top-level `lens` (`regulator`, `insurer`, `jv-partner` or `client`) for the auditor pack; `knowable_then` on timeline events. New profile rules: `no-owner-due`, `banner:<key>`, `knowable-then`, `nine-m-overview`, `nine-m-cites` and `m1-balance`, and `lint_errors`, which makes chosen language rules errors in one pack (JC5 in the crosswalk, JC3 and JC4 in the failure-to-learn review).
- **Extensions to existing skills.** `precursor-timeline` places the missed opportunities as events knowable then; `emergency-response-learnings` builds the incident day from `--event-day` with a gaps-between-steps table; `engineering-integrity-learnings` has an alarms-and-automation section; `manager-coach` covers work management (`select --prefix SVIO`); `auditor-assurance-pack` asks who the assurance is for and writes a `lens` section; `investigator-coach` carries the evidence scorecard; `organisation-learning-map` shows a nine-cell 9M strip and an optional 9M table; `learning-clusters` names the process-industries pack; `cluster-learning-dossier` reads every level within its 60k budget.
- **The register.** `nine_m` and `nine_m_subcategory` (copied from `nine-m-map.json`) become the "9M" and "9M subcategory" columns of the workbook, with a "Learnings by 9M and HFACS level" summary, when any learning has them.
- **evals.** 134 → 185 evals (69 trigger, 33 no-trigger, 39 collision, 44 router). Blind routing: a fresh agent that saw only the skill descriptions and the hub's routing table routed 70 of 70 prompts to the expected skill or preset, router evals 44 of 44 (floor 90%).
- **Tooling.** `tools/smoke_sample.py` fails when a pack skill has no Hazira golden in its `check-pack --strict` loop, so the nine new packs are checked and rendered in every smoke run. With Chrome on the machine (CI's Node leg and the local gate), a PNG test that cannot render fails instead of skipping (`PMLC_REQUIRE_PNG=1`). The skill counts in the tests and the documents follow the skill folders.
- **Migration notes.**
  - **`evidence_strength` is engine-checked.** When `evidence_audit.json` exists, `check-register` reports a learning whose `evidence_strength` differs from the engine's (`corroborated` needs at least 2 distinct evidence items of at least 2 types across its findings) as a problem: run `evidence-audit` and copy its value. Without the file nothing changes.
  - **Snippets end on whole words.** `timeline_candidates.json` snippets and `timeline-item` text are cut at a space, with `…` where cut, instead of mid-word. Packs that pasted the old snippets still pass; regenerate any reference copies.
  - **`artefacts.json` paths changed.** `evidence-audit` is now `workspace/evidence_audit.json` and `hfacs-profile` is `workspace/hfacs_profile.json`, both produced by the engine (they named the planned skill packs before); `nine-m-map` is `outputs/nine-m-map.json`, written by `pack`. `open-questions`, `nine-m-seed` and `missed-opportunities` are new, and no Phase 5 entry is `planned` any longer.
  - **The register has a 9M column.** `nine_m` and `nine_m_subcategory` are optional; a register without them writes the same workbook as before.
  - **Evals can live in fragment files.** `evals/run_evals.py` loads `evals/evals.json`, then every `evals/skills/<skill>.json` (`{"evals": [...]}`) in name order; ids are unique across the files and the floors apply to the union.
  - **No owners or due dates in three packs.** In `nine-m-analysis`, `recommendation-strength-review` and `risk-register-update`, an item with an `owner` or `by_when` is a problem even without `--strict` ("owner and due dates are set by the organisation, not in this pack").
  - **`timeline-item --before`** is no longer required by the parser; the command still needs it unless `--event-day` is given.
  - **Unchanged.** No flag, subcommand, pack field, kind, artefact name or output file name was renamed or removed. `pack` with no `--format` writes what it wrote in alpha.6, and alpha.6 and earlier packs still pass a non-strict `check-pack`. Without `--industry` the Hazira numbers do not move.

### 2.0.0a6

Phase 4: outputs.

- **Formats.** `pack --format` takes a comma-separated list; the default stays `html,md`. New formats: `docx` (Word, A4, citations as endnotes, status and page number in every footer), `pptx` (16:9 deck, graph slides as editable shapes, citations in the speaker notes), `email` (an inline-styled digest under 100 KB, with a 78-column `.txt` twin), `scorm` (a SCORM 1.2 course with a quiz graded in the browser), `gift`, `qti` and `quiz-csv` (the questions for Moodle, QTI 2.1 systems and spreadsheets), `svg`, `png` and `graphml` (each graph item; PNG through a local Chromium-family browser, `PMLC_BROWSER` first; when the browser writes no image, the engine retries once with the browser's sandbox switched off (`--no-sandbox`), and if that fails too the note gives both exit reasons), `bowtie-csv`, `ics` (follow-up reminders, only from an explicit `--start`), `csv` (the pack's actions, never overwritten), `srt` and `narration` (from a video or radio script) and `normalised` (the pack for other tools). A format with nothing to write in a pack says `<format>: nothing to write (<why>)` and exits 0. `pack -h` lists them all; `render-all --format` passes the list through.
- **Print layouts.** `pack --layout cards|field|toolbox|checklist|poster|paper` writes `<pack>.<layout>.html` beside the page: pocket or A4 cards (`--paper A4|A5`), field cards with end-notes and a printable delivery record, one A4 toolbox talk a page, tick-box checklists ending "Checked by (role)", a one-page A3 poster, and a board paper (with `--format docx`, also `<pack>.paper.docx`). Every printed page carries the status phrase, the short footer and, until sign-off, DRAFT. `--base-url` puts a QR code on each printed item (Project Nayuki's QR Code generator, MIT, vendored unmodified).
- **Learner view.** `--view learner` writes `<pack>.learner.<ext>` for every document format, without answers, good-evidence notes or correct-option flags, and with "Learner view: answers are hidden" under the status.
- **Translations.** New `strings extract` writes a pack's text as XLIFF 1.2 (`<pack>.strings.xlf`, the default) or CSV, with citations as placeholders and a back-translation unit for every safety-critical item; `strings apply --lang <tag>` writes `<pack>-<lang>.json`. Neither file is ever overwritten. Hindi (`hi`) labels and glossary translations ship, AI-drafted and marked for a fluent speaker's check; every translated page says so under the status. Right-to-left languages render mirrored.
- **Brand.** `pack --brand brand.json` sets the organisation's name, an accent colour (refused below a 4.5:1 contrast against white) and a logo (PNG or JPEG, at most 200 KB) on the page, the layouts, Word and PowerPoint. Any other key is refused: the footer and the status line are fixed. The email digest shows the organisation's name, without a logo. In a `--share external|public` copy the organisation's name is masked like any other organisation in the pack, and a public copy carries no logo.
- **The register in Word and email.** New `register-render --format docx,email` writes `learnings-register.docx` and `learnings-register.email.html` / `.txt`.
- **Readability and accessibility.** Every page, Markdown file, Word file, email and layout shows "About *n* min read" (200 words a minute). Pages have a skip link, keyboard focus and screen-reader labels, and are checked with axe-core (zero serious or critical findings on a pack page, every layout, the email, `index.html`, the dashboard and the SCORM page). The dashboard's matrix also reads as text.
- **New pack content (all optional).** The `toolbox` kind (opening question, story, two warning signs, one change, crew question) and the `script` kind (timed segments for video, radio or the PA); `injects` on a scenario; `did_you_know` and `what_you_can_do` on a card; `type` and `options` on a question; top-level `skill`, `lang`, `dir` and `translation_of`; `source_text` and `back_translation` on any item. An unknown kind now renders as a labelled field list instead of stopping the page.
- **Skills offer their formats.** Each skill's `skill.json` lists the formats it offers (`formats`), its "Check and hand over" section gives the `pack` line for each, and the hub turns a request for a file type or a channel (a Word paper, slides, printed sheets, a quiz, another language, calendar reminders) into the right flag. Every pack skill links its Hazira worked example.
- **Hazira samples.** The sample folder holds JSON, HTML and Markdown for every pack skill, a Hindi frontline pack, and one showcase of each other document format (Word and board paper, slides, email, cards, GIFT, the causal map as SVG and GraphML). The comparator inputs for `report-comparison` are committed beside the official report. Every sample is still a draft.
- **evals.** A `command_contains` expectation grades the `pmlc` command an agent ran (from `grade --transcript`). Twenty-five new trigger evals ask for a file type or a channel in a user's own words (a Word board paper, workshop slides, toolbox sheets, Hindi cards, A5 pocket cards, field cards with QR codes, a Moodle quiz, a SCORM course, a learner copy without answers, subtitles and a radio voice-over, a supervisors' hold-point checklist, drill inject cards and slides, the causal map as a picture or for yEd, a committee checklist and calendar reminders, the register in Word and email, executive and contractor email digests, engineering learnings in Word with an actions CSV, the barriers for bow-tie software, an audit checklist, a precursor poster, a department's dossier in Word, the regulator-safe comparison in Word, and the follow-up table as a spreadsheet). 134 evals in all; router evals stay 34 of 34. Routing measured blind (a fresh agent that saw only the skill descriptions, the presets, the hub's routing guidance and the format names): 58 of 58 prompts routed as expected, router evals 34 of 34 and the format evals 24 of 24, on the skill and on the format or layout name (floor 90%).
- **Tooling.** `tools/smoke_sample.py` now also checks every Hazira golden pack under `check-pack --strict` and renders it as html, md, docx, pptx, email and csv, checking each Word and PowerPoint file as a package. The test suite checks every format's conformance (status, credit and DRAFT in the right slot), every shared copy's masking and every fixture in every format.
- **Fixes.**
  - `register-items` gives each tier's items their own ids (`t3-lr-03`), so items from several tiers can sit in one pack.
  - `table-item --pack` drops the ids the pack excludes from its row cites; `check-pack` explains how to fix a finding that is both cited and excluded.
  - A shared copy's actions CSV carries no run slug.
  - The adoption, distribution and verification files are read back row by row: every bad row is reported and the good rows are kept.
  - The print layouts draw a table item once.
- **Migration notes.**
  - **The default formats are unchanged.** `pack` with no `--format` writes `<pack>.html` and `<pack>.md` (plus `follow-up.json` and `gaps.json` where they apply), as in alpha.5. Every new flag, field and kind is optional, and alpha.5, alpha.4 and v0.3 packs still pass a non-strict `check-pack` and render in every format.
  - **`cmd_pack` moved** from `pmlc_lib/pack/html.py` to `pmlc_lib/pack/command.py`. `pmlc.cmd_pack` and `pmlc_pack.cmd_pack` still resolve, and `render_pack_html` and `render_pack_md` keep their names, modules and signatures.
  - **Every pack's HTML and Markdown change.** The reading-time line is new in both; the HTML also gains the skip link and right-to-left-safe CSS (logical properties). A tool that compares rendered pages byte for byte with alpha.5 output will see these differences; regenerate its reference copies.
  - **`options` on a `question`** is now a list of `{"text": ..., "correct": true|false}` objects, used with `type`. alpha.5 ignored `options` on a question as an unknown field; a list of plain strings there is now a problem. `options` on a `decision` stays a list of strings.
  - **The organisation's files are read back row by row.** A bad row in `adoption.csv`, `distribution.csv` or `verification.jsonl` used to stop the read; now each bad row is reported (a warning on the dashboard and on stderr) and the good rows are kept. `y` and `n` count as `yes` and `no`.
  - **`register-items` ids are tier-prefixed**: `t3-lr-03`, not `lr-03`. Packs that pasted the old ids still pass; new pastes use the new ids.
  - **`table-item --pack` drops excluded ids** from `row_cites` and `cites`, so a row can end with an empty cite list.
  - **A shared actions CSV has no run slug** in its ids (`<pack>/<item-id>`); the internal file keeps it.
  - **`render.register()` raises** `ValueError` when a second function registers an existing format name. Only code that registers its own formats is affected.
  - **New caps in `pack-profiles.json`** can turn `check-pack --strict` results into problems for packs above them: pack word limits (`max_words`: board-brief 1500, top-management-brief 2000, dossier-F02 9000); auditor-assurance-pack evidence requests up to 20; investigator-coach interview plan up to 31 (six roles of five, plus the validation question); manager-coach paragraphs up to 3 and recommendations up to 4 in each section; site-leadership-coach visible checks up to 6; top-management-brief policy paragraphs up to 8 and assurance questions up to 6; and the new optional sections, frontline toolbox talks and training scripts, up to 3 each. Without `--strict` they are warnings.
  - **A pack's `skill`, when present, is checked**: `pack` must be that skill's pack or a variant of it (`<pack>-<variant>`), else `pack <p> is not a pack or variant of skill <s>`.
  - **evals: 109 → 134.** Router evals stay 34 of 34.
  - **The sample outputs grow** from about 2.5 MB to about 23 MB (about 4.6 MB compressed per regeneration in git), because every pack skill's golden HTML page is committed (ruling OD4-5).
  - **The JavaScript tests need npm packages.** Contributors who run the tests with Node.js installed run `npm ci --prefix tests/js` once (axe-core 4.10.2 and jsdom 25.0.1, test-only); without it the accessibility tests fail on purpose with `run: npm ci --prefix tests/js`. Without Node.js they skip.

### 2.0.0a5

Phase 3: the shared spine and the planner.

- **The planner.** New `plan <ws> --workflow <preset>` (or `--audiences`, `--skills`) turns a request into a numbered plan of steps, with each step's commands and gate, and writes `plan.json`. `plan --next` prints the next step to do. Nine workflow presets ship: `first-48-hours`, `full-cascade`, `leadership`, `assurance`, `investigation-quality`, `action-design`, `sister-sites`, `training` and `follow-up`.
